What was changed and how selection worked

Guarded IDs encode nominal basket loss budget r5/r10 = 5%/10% of starting closed balance; g100/g300 = 10/30-pip spacing; tp0.25/tp0.5/tp1 = 0.25R/0.5R/1R basket targets. Fixed equal lots within each basket, initially 0.10 at r5 or 0.20 at r10 per $10k, proportionally scaled at the next basket start. Maximum 5 orders, no forced alternating sides, 72-hour maximum age. Entry remains EMA5 plus three strictly increasing closed M5 tick volumes.

Supplemental fast IDs also state initial equal lots per $10k: 0.5/1.0 at 5% budget and 1.0/2.0 at 10% budget. They retain 10-pip spacing, 5-order cap, 72-hour exit and the same signals. This higher exposure allows shorter price moves to reach a given cash target, but also reaches cash stops sooner.

Development uses 2020-10-10 → 2024-10-10. Gate: ≥30 baskets, positive return, basket PF ≥1.05, no stopout, equity DD ≤70%. Rank by compounded equivalent monthly growth, then PF, then lower DD. The initial group selects three candidates and the supplemental group two; if too few pass, remaining slots use the highest-ranked gate failures, clearly labelled exploratory. Each selection file is frozen before its validation. No validation-result retuning.

Mean month is the arithmetic mean of complete calendar-month floating-equity returns; median shows the typical month. Compound/mo is the full-window equivalent monthly growth from final account value, not an arithmetic average; it is undefined (N/A) when the account ends at or below zero. Boundary partial October months are listed but excluded from monthly mean, median and 10% frequency. No-trade months are retained. Full-window total return and drawdown include those partial months, so a positive complete-month mean can coexist with a negative total return. A 10% month here or there does not demonstrate a 10% average. Different independent starting dates can change grid paths.

Recent prices were already examined in earlier raw work; this is selection-excluded validation, NOT genuinely untouched OOS. Older tick history contains generated ticks; native real-tick percentages are disclosed per case. DD is the larger of native MT5 and before/after trade tick-observer measurements. Equity graphs sample one minute and may miss transient minima. Historical outcomes are not future-return promises.
