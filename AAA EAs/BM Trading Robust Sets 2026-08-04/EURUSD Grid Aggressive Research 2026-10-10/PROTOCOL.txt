EXPLORATORY HIGH-RISK EURUSD GRID STUDY — NOT LIVE DEPLOYMENT
Frozen candidate list before results. Aim: assess 10% average monthly return, NOT guarantee it.

Native MT5, EURUSD M5, $10,000 USD, Exness-MT5Trial16 hedging, leverage 1:2000, real-tick Model4 with broker generated-tick fallback, 150ms delay, broker bid/ask/commission/swap. No deposits or top-ups. Stopouts and final losing baskets remain in results.

Original stress tests: unchanged EMA5 + three increasing closed-bar tick volumes, 300 points/30 pips spacing, arithmetic grid, forced alternating baskets, no SL/cap/time exit. Scale starting lot, lot increment AND $10 basket target together by 5x, 10x, 20x. Fixed lots, not percent risk. Run each on 1 year and 3 years. These are risk-scaling diagnostics, not optimisation or holdout tests.

Guarded research derivative: same signal and absolute nearest-entry spacing. Fixed equal lot size within each basket, max 5 filled orders, no forced alternation, max basket age 72 hours. Starting lot at $10k is 0.10 for 5% budget, 0.20 for 10% budget, scaled proportionally to CLOSED balance when a new basket starts; floor broker lot step, skip below minimum. Budget frozen at 5% or 10% of basket starting balance. EA attempts market close when estimated net liquidation value reaches minus the budget. This is a client-side cash exit, NOT a guaranteed maximum loss or broker-held stop. Gaps/fees/rejection can exceed it. Basket profit target = 0.25R, 0.5R or 1R of that budget. Grid spacing = 100 or 300 native points (10/30 pips). All combinations: 2 risk budgets x 3 targets x 2 spacings = 12 finite candidates.

Development: 2020-10-10 to 2024-10-10 exclusive. Rank candidates without any later history: first require >=30 baskets, positive return, basket PF >=1.05, no stopout and max floating-equity DD <=70%. Select top 3 by compounded equivalent monthly growth, then PF, then lower DD. If fewer than 3 pass, report gate failure and fill remaining slots with best other candidates, clearly labelled FAILED DEVELOPMENT GATE; do not silently replace failed candidates using later results.

Freeze selection before validation. Test those 3 settings on 2024-10-10 to 2026-10-10 (latest 2 years), and an independent fresh-account last-year run. Earlier raw work has already exposed parts of recent history: this is parameter-selection-excluded validation, NOT genuinely untouched OOS. No repeat tuning on validation outcomes.

Report calendar-month floating-equity percentage returns, arithmetic mean, median, equivalent compounded monthly growth, 10%+ month frequency, positive month count, worst month, PF/order and basket WR, tick-floating DD, stopout events, min observed equity, maximum lots/orders, unfinished baskets. Partial boundary October months are explicitly labelled and excluded from monthly averages/frequency; total/CAGR metrics use the full requested window. Equity monthly snapshots use final available tick per UTC calendar month. Keep all no-trade months. Final test liquidation included. Preserve individual orders and full baskets separately.

Both source versions refuse live execution. No live terminal API, existing EAs, BATs, website or Git push. No claim that selecting from these candidates establishes a new robust edge. Full genuine real-tick history is unavailable in older years; disclose native tick-quality percentages.
