Research ORB regime portfolio

Breakouts + failed-break reversals · US100 and gold CFDs · 1% equity-risk target · research only

Live EAs, BATs, presets, website and active MT5 account remain unchanged. No options surface or exchange order-flow data was fabricated. This is a new adaptation, not a claim to reproduce someone’s undisclosed strategy.

Decision

No tested version passes every promotion check. Gold’s RVOL-filtered failed-break reversal is the strongest two-year risk-adjusted research candidate: +16.32%, PF 1.59, 55.3% wins, 76 trades, native floating DD 6.55% and daily-equity Sharpe 1.15. Its last year returned +9.24%, but the last three months lost 0.88% on only seven trades. Earlier validation returned −9.37% (PF 0.59). The 10,000-path historical bootstrap has 92.06% profitable paths, below the 95% gate, with a negative fifth-percentile return. Keep it research-only.

The two-module controller uses past-only daily Markov routing; adding breakout days did not improve gold’s result (hybrid +4.96%, PF 1.12) or US100’s result (hybrid −15.48%, PF 0.79). No ATR/range-normalisation/directional-filter breakout finalist earned promotion, so the unchanged plain breakout remains the comparison control. These findings do not establish a profitable institutional volatility-surface ORB.

Research basis and what is missing

Realised ATR and CFD tick-volume proxies are available. No licensed historical option chain/surface, skew, convexity, term structure, bid/ask aggressor delta or futures exchange volume is present. The added Markov gate is not an institutional order-flow model.

University of St Gallen / SFI ORB working paper — Opening relative volume and ATR are research hypotheses; stock-universe results do not establish CFD profitability. This is a working paper, not a claimed peer-reviewed guarantee.

Lundstrom, Day trading returns across volatility states — Volatility-dependent ORB hypothesis; not an exact replication of the paper.

CME CVOL overview — Actual surface features include skew, convexity and ATM volatility. Historical licensed data are not present; these features were NOT fabricated or used.

Exness server clock documentation — Exness documents GMT+0 server time. The EA converts UTC to New York wall clock using US DST rules. Gold uses the 09:30 NY session anchor; this is not a claim that gold itself opens at the stock-market bell.
