# D14 / H1 / M5 break-and-retest — frozen raw specification

Frozen before any result is inspected. One identical rule set for XAUUSD and USTEC (US100), no parameter search, no live installation. The video transcript is discretionary and incomplete; this is an explicitly defined interpretation, not a claim of exact replication.

1. At each broker daily bar, use only the preceding 14 completed D1 candles. Compare the high and low of the newest seven with the high and low of the older seven. Both higher = buy bias; both lower = sell bias; otherwise flat/no entries. No moving averages or optimized trend threshold.
2. A swing is a strict fractal with two completed bars to its left and two to its right, on both H1 and M5. It is unavailable until the second right-hand bar closes. Equal highs/lows do not qualify.
3. H1 buy zone: a confirmed swing high followed by a confirmed swing low, then the first H1 close crossing above that high. The broken high candle's upper wick is the zone: max(open, close) to high. A zero-width wick is expanded to one tradable tick. Sells mirror this using the lower wick of a broken swing low after a swing high.
4. Only the latest qualifying zone is active. A new zone replaces the previous one. A daily bias change clears the zone. An M5 close beyond its far edge invalidates it. Do not reconstruct stale pre-test zones; wait for a newly observed H1 break. One attempted entry per zone, one open position per symbol.
5. Beginning after zone creation, wait for an M5 candle to touch the zone. Require a three-candle holding window with at least two rejection wicks: for a buy, the low is in the zone and the full candle body is above its upper edge; mirror for sells. All three candles must be complete and from/after the first touch. The window can slide until valid or the zone is invalidated.
6. After holding is confirmed, wait for a newly confirmed M5 swing high, then a newly confirmed swing low higher than the holding-window low. Enter long on a subsequent completed M5 close crossing above that swing high, at the next available tick's ask. Mirror for shorts (low, lower high, close through low, bid entry). Breakouts before the swing is confirmed are not retroactively traded.
7. Stop one tradable tick below the final M5 swing low (above swing high for sells). Target twice the requested executable-entry-to-stop distance, rounded to a tradable price tick. Actual fill gaps/delay can change realized RR. No trailing, break-even, partial exits, ATR exits, news filter or portfolio/adaptive controls.
8. No session cutoff or forced same-day exit was specified, so none is invented. Positions can last overnight; native recorded swap is included. Tester-boundary liquidations are labelled separately.
9. Independent $10,000 USD account per symbol and per window, 1% current-equity planned stop risk; lot step rounds up, minimum lot allowed. Thus actual risk can exceed 1%. Commission is not included in the sizing budget; it is counted in realized net results. Broker minimum stop/margin constraints can prevent an order. No martingale/grid.
10. Native isolated Exness MT5 tester, real-tick mode requested (actual coverage audited), same broker feed as earlier raw research. Period ends 2026-09-05 exclusive: 6m from 2026-03-05, 1y from 2025-09-05, 3y from 2023-09-05, 5y from 2021-09-05. Each window restarts balance and strategy state; the windows are overlapping, not independent samples.

All assumptions remain frozen across the runs. Fix implementation errors if found, but do not tune the strategy based on its returns. Strategy tester is distinct from live account execution. Cash results are broker-feed-specific historical simulations, not guarantees or verified FTMO results.
