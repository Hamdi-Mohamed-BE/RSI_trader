Reconstructed rules

  1. Completed M5 tick volumes must strictly increase: V[1] > V[2] > V[3]. Buy when the latest closed bar is above EMA5(close); sell below it. Both sides use increasing volume.
  2. One evaluation per newly completed candle. First fill occurs on the next available tick; stale pre-weekend bars are not used as fresh signals.
  3. One directional basket at a time. New same-side signals add only when the executable quote is at least 300 points from every existing entry. Absolute spacing permits additions in either price direction. On five-digit EURUSD this is 30 pips. Execution latency may change final fill spacing.
  4. Lots: 0.01, 0.02, 0.03, …, rounded down to broker step. No doubling formula or hidden maximum-order stop is invented.
  5. Close the entire basket at an estimated $10 net liquidation profit, reserving opening commission/fees again for expected exit costs. Market close slippage means realised cash need not equal exactly $10. No individual SL/TP, trailing or time exit.
  6. After the entire basket closes, seek the opposite side. An initial basket can start in either direction.

M5 timeframe, lot increment, cash target, completed-bar signal evaluation, absolute nearest-order spacing and forced alternation are explicit reconstruction choices, not recovered original code. Tick volume is broker tick activity, not consolidated FX traded volume.
