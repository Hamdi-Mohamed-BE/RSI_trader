Exact frozen rules and assumptions
  • test_type: Unoptimised single-index reconstruction, not a stock-universe replication
  • symbol: Exness USTEC (US100 CFD)
  • start: 2020-01-01
  • end_exclusive: 2023-01-01
  • initial_balance_usd: 10000
  • risk_percent: 1
  • range: 09:30 inclusive to 09:35 exclusive America/New_York, five completed M1 bid bars
  • relative_volume: Opening five-minute tick volume / arithmetic mean of previous 14 exchange sessions' opening five-minute tick volume
  • minimum_relative_volume: 2
  • direction: Opening range candle close > open: buy stop at bid range high. Close < open: sell stop at bid range low. Equal: skip.
  • entry: First ask touch of buy stop or bid touch of sell stop after 09:35; adverse opening gap filled at current quote; maximum one trade per exchange session
  • atr: Arithmetic mean of true ranges of the previous 14 completed New York cash sessions, including overnight close-to-session gaps
  • stop: 0.10 times lagged daily ATR14, from execution price, rounded to 0.01 index point
  • exit: Stop or New York cash close (16:00 ET, 13:00 on official early-close days); no target, trailing, break-even or other filters
  • primary_execution: Conservative M1 OHLC: entry-bar high/low ordering ambiguous cases stop first. Bid bars + historical per-minute spread; stop gaps fill adversely.
  • ordering_sensitivity: Alternative feasible entry-bar ordering ignores pre-entry opposite extreme unless close breaches stop; otherwise identical
  • missing_spread: Zero/missing spread replaced only by last previously observed positive spread; no future backfill
  • sizing: Floor to 0.01 lot; 0.05 minimum, 500 maximum; $1/index point/lot from saved broker specification
  • commission: Zero explicit commission assumed. Additional 2.0 index-point round-trip execution-cost sensitivity also reported. No swaps for same-session trades.
  • data_quality: Skip sessions with incomplete opening range, missing prior-14-session inputs, cash-session gaps, or no quote within two minutes of the cash close. No data imputation for prices or volume.
  • not_included: Cross-sectional top-20 ranking, stock-specific liquidity filters, real tick sequencing, spread changes within a minute, latency, queue position, margin/liquidity/stop rejection modelling
  • optimisation: False
  • live_changes: False
