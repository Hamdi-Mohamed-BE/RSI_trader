Limitations

The hourly selection and sizing references use the same recent evidence; no untouched holdout. Standalone settings can differ from FTMO inputs (see Results.json). Baseline planned risk is estimated from cached rows, not independently reconstructed initial order stops. Multi-module and partial-close state is simplified to one open cached trade per EA. Naive tester timestamps are treated as UTC. Historical costs are retained with explicit cost floors, not historical FTMO quotes. Missing warm-start trades are excluded. Margin uses simplified 1:15 index/metals, 1:30 FX; broker specifications can differ. Admission rejections can change future strategy state, which a cached-ledger replay cannot reproduce. No intratrade mark-to-market, margin-call, gap-exit or true live execution simulation. Reserve stress tests are scenarios, not a mathematical upper/lower bracket.
