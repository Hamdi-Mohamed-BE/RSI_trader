# Evidence and modeling disclosures

The full current installer lists 34 products. A terminal/account connection was confirmed read-only (Exness demo), but the saved chart profile does not prove that every catalogue EA is attached in the current session or on a remote VPS. Therefore the broad basket means the current installer catalogue, not a verified list of 34 running robots.

One EA, Gold News V9 Direction, has no comparable native historical ledger. The 33 remaining catalogue EAs have audited entry stops and cash arithmetic in older cached reports. **31 of those report binary hashes differ from today's binaries**, and XAU Regime Switch also differs in SET hash. Shared risk-control rebuilds may explain some changes but source parity is NOT proven. Their output is a historical strategy-basket sensitivity, not a completed current-build 34-EA audit.

The newest FTMO13 evidence was separately reused and its package/source hashes were rechecked unchanged. It covers 2025-09-27 to 2026-09-25 exclusive, 363 days, with 972 source trades. The broad common cached year covers 2025-08-31 to 2026-08-31 exclusive. No missing September observations were invented. No bootstrap or claim of future pass probability is made.

The source total swap is spread proportionally across its observed holding duration for marked-equity calculations. This uses ex-post cost information, not exact historical rollover charges. Native final realized costs remain preserved; a forced early exit receives prorated carry. It is a research cost-timing approximation, not fully point-in-time cost validation. Stress doubles negative swaps and adds round-trip hypothetical adverse movement: gold $0.20, Nasdaq 2 points, USDJPY 0.02, BTC $30, ETH $3, silver $0.04, EURUSD 0.0002. It does not model order-book failures or a worst-case market.

Public FTMO commissions are conservatively treated as per-side where applied; native commission is a floor, not an additional double charge. FTMO target lot minimum/step 0.01 is assumed in target contract units; ETH's 10-unit contract is converted to source 1-unit units. Actual FTMO account minimums, historical spreads and swaps are not available. Normal-account margin is extrapolated from read-only current Exness order_calc_margin snapshots; historical high-margin requirements around news are not reconstructed.

Minute equity excludes pre-entry bar extremes. Native exits within a minute are recognized at the next minute boundary, so subminute daily classification and the trajectory of quick news trades are unresolved. A goal/loss signal schedules liquidation one minute later at the first observed minute open; original native exits can occur first. Within-minute adverse prices across instruments are not simultaneous and are diagnostic only. Native exits override reconstructed prices. Some older exit fills lie outside current M1 spread estimates; the latest FTMO13 sample has none farther than 0.25R outside the matching bar envelope, but this does not establish exact tick equivalence.

The broad News Pulse sensitivity does NOT reserve unfilled opposite-side pending orders. This can overstate available margin/risk capacity, particularly for approach B, and together with hindsight-fit news parameters makes the all-news headline returns unsuitable for deployment decisions. The separate non-news comparison exposes this distortion. Gold News V9 is excluded, not assumed inactive or risk-free.

The comparison substitutes the requested fixed-risk/admission rules for existing adaptive/BAT governors. It does not claim to reproduce the current installer’s full guard stack. Signal lists are fixed: after rejected/early-closed trades, reentry/state paths are not regenerated. A deployable implementation must coordinate all EAs and pending orders and be tested in a true shared-account simulation.

Additional predeclared sensitivities: (1) 0.5% current marked equity per trade while daily dollar thresholds stay fixed to initial capital; (2) five-minute instead of one-minute forced-close delay; (3) a single historical 2-Step path with four opening days each phase, passage only when flat, reset to $10K and next-business-day restart. The latter uses an optimistic zero-review-delay assumption and no payouts. It is not an estimated pass probability or promise of funded cash.
