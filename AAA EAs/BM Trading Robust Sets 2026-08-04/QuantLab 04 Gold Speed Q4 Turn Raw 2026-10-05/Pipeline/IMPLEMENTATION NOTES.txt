Audit notes, 2026-10-05:
- Native optimisation uses multiple LOCAL tester cores inside one isolated research terminal. These are not autonomous subagents; no cloud/remote tester agents and no live terminal API.
- Pending limit/stop budgets are calculated at placement using then-current equity; requested cash is exported and actual filled stop cash separately recorded. Account changes while pending can change percentage at fill. Orders expire after4 signal-timeframe bars. One scheduled order/module, no pyramiding.
- Filters/side restrictions apply to the signal before a confirmation-candle wait. They are not recomputed after the confirmation candle. This is the exact tested convention, not an intrabar regime predictor.
- C records one calendar attempt after reaching its configured entry hour and broker session. Added session/EMA/calendar filters may veto that attempt for the month instead of retrying later. Session variants on this calendar module are rejection gates at the entry attempt, not rewritten session-opening entries. A/B session variants genuinely restrict signal entry times. This limits C optimisation coverage; no claim of a global optimum.
- Day-entry cap counts scheduled orders, including subsequently expired pending orders. This is conservative and not a fill-count cap.
- D1 signal variants retain finite completed-bar warmup requirements; low-count/unavailable-bounds variants are visible and excluded by counts, not treated as profitable zero-trade systems.
- If a sample has positive P&L but zero losses, search metrics cap undefined PF at99 for ranking; this is a sentinel, NOT a measured exact PF99. Bootstrap zero-loss samples use the same cap. Inspect trade counts and uncertainty.
- Partial50% at1R is skipped when lot-step/minimum constraints prevent two legal remaining portions. The original full position then continues. Latest high-volatility gold periods can skip entry entirely if the smallest lot is over budget; never auto-raise risk to minimum lot.
- Trace plots are sampled5-minute equity and can miss extrema; table maxEQDD comes from native MT5 tick statistics. Closed-cash MC/DSR and calendar contribution curves are not funded-account safety guarantees.
- Same-currency broker price-distance stops are distinct from account-riskUSD. Fixed $5/$10/$20 stop variants here mean XAUUSD price displacement, not $5/$10/$20 cash exposure.
