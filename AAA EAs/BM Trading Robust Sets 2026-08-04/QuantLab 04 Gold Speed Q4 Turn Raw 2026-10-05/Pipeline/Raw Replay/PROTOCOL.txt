A - Adaptive speed momentum H4 (ALL OUR numerical rules):
- ATR14 = simple average true ranges on completed H4.
- Fast mode when current ATR14 > mean of latest 50 completed ATR14 values.
- Fast momentum/breakout lookback 6 H4 bars; slow mode lookback 24 H4 bars.
- Long: signal close minus close[lookback] > 0.5*ATR14, EMA100 rising,
  signal close > highest HIGH of prior lookback bars (excluding signal).
- Short: mirrored.
- SL 2.5*ATR14; no TP.
- From +1 initial R, trail at original stop distance behind best completed-bar high/low;
  tighten only on H4 close, valid current quote and open broker session required.

B - Q4 breakout H1 (ALL OUR numerical rules):
- New entries in Oct/Nov/Dec ONLY, determined by entry tick date. Existing trades held until own exit.
- Current ATR14 > mean latest 50 completed ATR14 values.
- Long: completed close breaks HIGH of prior 60 H1 bars AND lies in top 10% of prior 480-bar high-low range.
- Short: lower 60-bar low and bottom 10% of same range.
- SL 2*ATR14; TP2.5R.
- Trail from +1 initial R at original stop distance behind best completed H1 extreme; tighten only on H1 close.

C - Turn-of-month H1 scheduling, weekday calendar (ALL OUR numerical rules):
- Long first eligible tick of last Monday-Friday day of month.
- One entry attempt per last weekday date, including if stopped. Holidays NOT predicted/modelled in calendar.
- SL 2*D1 ATR14 from completed daily candles; no TP or trail.
- Exit within last 15 minutes of trade session on second Monday-Friday date of next month.
- If missed/non-tradable, retry at first eligible tick after scheduled date; max ten calendar days safeguard.
- Intraday timing runs each tick; H1 is descriptive scheduling, not an H1 directional signal.
