Nasdaq DI OFF versus ON

CORE3 and CORE4 · one fresh $10,000 shared account per start · fixed-$50-capped, down-rounded current guard model.

History: 6 October 2025–6 October 2026, Prague midnight boundaries. The same 134 weekday starts, 6 October 2025–9 April 2026, each have 180 Prague calendar days total for both phases. Another 127 late weekday starts are excluded.

DI OFF reduced completion, without a typical paired timing gain

CORE3 completed both phases on 133/134 starts with DI ON versus 125/134 with DI OFF. CORE4 went from 134/134 to 126/134. The eight extra non-completers in each DI-off basket remained pending—not hard-failed—at the 180-day horizon.

Among identical starts where both DI versions qualify, the median paired OFF − ON timing difference is 0 days for both baskets. Keep DI ON as the stronger choice in this particular estimated replay.

Worst sampled equity drawdown across primary cohorts also rose: CORE3 $377.19 → $725.87; CORE4 $353.81 → $737.16. These are model-observed cash amounts, not certified maximum intratick losses.

These are overlapping, retrospectively selected historical cohorts using sampled floating equity—not independent experiments, untouched out-of-sample evidence, or forecasts of your future FTMO pass probability. Rules, model limits and interpretation

  • Phase 1 requires naturally flat balance of $11,000; Phase 2 starts fresh at $10,000 and requires $10,500. Each phase needs four distinct Prague entry days. See FTMO’s official trading objectives.
  • The analytical 180-day horizon covers both phases and the handover; it is not an FTMO deadline. Primary Phase 2 starts next Prague midnight; the secondary model waits two weekdays. Actual review/KYC or funded access is unknown; see FTMO’s review and access FAQ.
  • Sizing retains the source request as a cap and floors the protected-stop budget to broker lot steps, up to $50 per entry. A nominal budget is not a guaranteed realized-loss ceiling.
  • The model includes $225 aggregate/$150 per-symbol initial risk, a persistent $300 daily entry stop, $9,200 admission buffer, seven entries and three completed losses per Prague day, and a conservative margin proxy. Hard floors are $9,000 static equity and Prague day-start balance minus $500. Equality is conservatively treated as a breach.
  • Floating equity uses causal, uniquely owned five-minute marks. A new position starts with zero floating until its first interior mark; no future marks are interpolated or backfilled. Carried marks may remain stale over weekends. Intraminute excursions and genuine broker margin/stop-management state are not reconstructed.
  • Immutable historical opportunities and exits are reused. A guard-skipped trade could change future signals in a true live/native replay; these counterfactual signals are unavailable. This is an estimated shared-account replay, not full native FTMO execution.
  • Recorded Exness costs include zero-spread archive requests, not certified FTMO costs. Stress adds per side USTEC 1 point, USDJPY 0.01 JPY and XAUUSD $0.10, and doubles negative recorded swap. It does not re-execute stops and exits on stressed quotes.
  • Long source reports say 15% real ticks across their five-year run, with real ticks beginning 1 January 2026; this percentage is not the real-tick fraction of this one-year slice. The 71-start 2026 sensitivity is predeclared, not newly optimized.
  • Control sources were taken from the previously saved study; their pre-existing cache identity limitation is retained. The DI-off Nasdaq source is freshly generated and independently audited. New-source identity does not cure the other model and historical-selection limits.
  • Counts, phase timing, cash and input checks verify the implementation—not the trading edge. Starts overlap heavily; no independent binomial confidence interval or future pass-probability claim is made.
