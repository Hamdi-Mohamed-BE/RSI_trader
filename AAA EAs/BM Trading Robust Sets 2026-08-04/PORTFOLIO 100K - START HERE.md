# USD 100,000 adaptive portfolio

**INSTALL AND RUN ON 100K MT5.bat** calls the same any-balance twelve-bot installer. At the default 1% setting, each EA targets approximately USD 1,000 at its planned stop on a USD 100,000 account.

The portfolio contains LTA Volume Profile, ATR Candle Breakout, Asia Breakout, DmC, Go Long, EMA3, XAU Weakness, Ninja Turtle, Nasdaq Overnight, Turnaround Tuesday, US100 Weakness and the force-enabled News Pulse test.

The results came from independent one-year tests, not a shared-account simulation. Twelve simultaneous positions could create approximately 12% planned exposure, and gaps or correlations can produce more.
