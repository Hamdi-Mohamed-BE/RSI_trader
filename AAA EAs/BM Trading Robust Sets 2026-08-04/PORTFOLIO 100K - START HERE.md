# USD 100,000 adaptive portfolio

**INSTALL AND RUN ON 100K MT5.bat** calls the same any-balance eleven-bot installer. At the default 1% setting, each EA targets approximately USD 1,000 at its planned stop on a USD 100,000 account.

The portfolio contains LTA Volume Profile, ATR Candle Breakout, Asia Breakout, DmC, Go Long, EMA3, XAU Weakness, Ninja Turtle, Nasdaq Overnight, Turnaround Tuesday and US100 Weakness.

The results came from independent one-year tests, not a shared-account simulation. Eleven simultaneous positions could create approximately 11% planned exposure, and gaps or correlations can produce more.
