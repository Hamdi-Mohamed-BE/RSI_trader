# Robot Trading Playbook — objective research translation

This is a research-only translation of the supplied transcript. It is not in the active BAT installer.

## What the speaker described

1. Continuation after a candle closes beyond resistance or support.
2. A stop entry above/below the confirming candle after a wick forms.
3. A “starter play”: an opposite-colour candle holds the broken level, followed by an entry through that candle's extreme.
4. A failed breakout that reclaims the old range in the higher-timeframe direction.
5. A pullback to newly formed support/resistance followed by continuation.

## Required mechanical definitions

- Resistance/support is the highest high/lowest low of a fixed number of fully closed signal-timeframe bars.
- A breakout is a close beyond that level by a fixed ATR buffer.
- Higher-timeframe bias is the fast/slow EMA relationship, with price on the trend side of the fast EMA.
- Entries are stop orders beyond the confirming candle's extreme.
- Stops are beyond that candle's opposite extreme plus an ATR buffer.
- Targets are a fixed multiple of initial risk.
- “Starter” and retest setups expire after a fixed number of bars.
- A fakeout is a close beyond the old range followed immediately by a directional close back inside it.

These definitions are necessary because the transcript does not define resistance construction, wick thresholds, target selection, or daily bias mathematically. Results apply only to this objective translation, not every discretionary interpretation of the video.
