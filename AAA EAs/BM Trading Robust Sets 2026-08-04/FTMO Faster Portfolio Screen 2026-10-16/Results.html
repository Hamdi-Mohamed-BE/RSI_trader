A faster portfolio without relaxing the historical completion gate

Twelve fixed add/remove baskets, registered before their replay outcomes. One fresh $10,000 shared account per weekday start; protected initial-stop sizing capped at $50 per entry, down-rounded to broker steps.

Latest matching year: 6 October 2025–6 October 2026, Prague midnight boundaries. The same 134 eligible starts, 6 October 2025–9 April 2026, each receive 180 Prague civil days total for both phases. Another 127 late weekday starts are excluded.

Nine EAs were fastest among the 12 registered baskets

Keep all four CORE4 EAs and add the five existing 0.5R ORBs. This nine-EA basket met all eight historical gates: 134/134 completions in each main scenario and 71/71 in each 2026-start scenario. Worst-scenario all-start 50% completion time improved from 141 to 119 days.

With recorded costs and next-midnight handover, its whole-attempt median was 99.5 days versus CORE4’s 127. The fair matched comparison is a 19-day median improvement across the same 134 starts—not the 27.5-day difference between those two separate medians.

A ≥75% historical completion threshold is not a ≥75% future pass probability. The baskets are selected on already-inspected history, the rolling starts overlap heavily, and five-minute equity marks miss intraminute risk. No live portfolio is changed by this report.

Frozen admission gate

Every one of four cost/handover scenarios must complete both phases on at least 101/134 starts. The 2026-start sensitivity must also reach at least 54/71 in every scenario. Pending and failed attempts both stay in the denominator.

Frozen speed criterion

Minimize the worst scenario’s all-start day reaching 50% completion. Tie-break by worst conditional whole-attempt median, fewer EAs, then stable basket ID. No ROI maximization, new combinations or relaxed gates after results.

    Rules and limits that matter

    • Phase 1 requires naturally flat balance ≥$11,000. Phase 2 starts fresh at $10,000 and requires ≥$10,500. Each needs four distinct Prague entry days. Qualification is completion of trading phases, not an actual funded-account activation; official FTMO objectives.
    • Primary Phase 2 starts next Prague midnight. The secondary two-weekday wait is a separate replay, not an arithmetic adjustment to an earlier pass date. The 180-day cutoff is analytical, not an FTMO deadline; actual review/KYC/access timing is unknown. Official review/access FAQ.
    • The entry model retains each source’s risk-sized request as a cap, then floors lots under the $50 initial-stop budget. It includes $225 aggregate / $150 symbol initial risk, a persistent $300 daily entry stop, $9,200 admission buffer, seven entries and three completed losses per Prague day, plus a margin proxy. Hard floors are $9,000 static equity and Prague day-start balance − $500; equality is treated conservatively as a breach.
    • Five-minute floating marks are causal and uniquely owned. Initial floating is zero until the first interior mark; stale past marks can carry through closures. There is no future interpolation, but missing intraminute excursions, active stop state and true broker margin can change outcomes. This is not a simultaneous native FTMO test.
    • Short-duration source positions can have no interior five-minute mark at all. In this matching-year source pool: Gold Value Area37/203; US100 H1 ORB8/77; Gold Overlap ORB9/26; US100 New York ORB2/15; Gold New York ORB2/12. Such accepted lifecycles retain zero modeled floating until exit. These are source-pool counts, not counts admitted by every portfolio replay.
    • Immutable source fills/exits cannot reconstruct all counterfactual signals after a skipped trade. Recorded Exness quotes/fees—including zero-spread archive requests—are execution proxies, not certified FTMO costs.
    • Stress adds per side USTEC 1 point, USDJPY 0.01 JPY and XAUUSD $0.10, doubling negative recorded swap; it does not re-execute entries/exits on stressed quotes. Older source history includes generated ticks; reports’ 15% real-tick figure covers their full five-year runs, not this one-year slice. The 2026-start subgroup is a sensitivity, not fresh out-of-sample validation.
    • Only the 12 registered baskets are tested. CORE3 is already CORE4 minus RSI, so it is not duplicated. No full 17-EA or 16-EA claim is made: the optional larger reference was prospectively omitted for source-model compatibility, and 3-Way Gold’s partial/overlap lifecycles are outside this screen. These were not exclusions after inspecting their performance.
    • Audit passes establish source, cash, phase and selection arithmetic consistency—not a durable trading edge. Searching even a small roster menu creates selection bias. Overlapping cohorts are dependent; no IID binomial forecast confidence intervals are presented.
