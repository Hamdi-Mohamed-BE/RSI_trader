# Raw Gold + XAU/XAG News: two-month FTMO feasibility study

Research only. No live trading, terminal/account changes, optimization, website or BAT changes.

- Window: 19 July 2026 inclusive to 19 September 2026 exclusive. Initial USD 10,000; one shared account and one attempt.
- Strategies: raw Gold Overnight Value Area; approved full-year-fitted event-specific News Pulse XAU and XAG. Entries/exits come from saved native Exness MT5 tests, not FTMO tests. Gold uses its 150ms run; news uses either the saved 1ms run or the separate 250ms sensitivity run. Source bid/ask spread, commissions and swaps remain in the ledger.
- Fixed-reference sizing for this comparison: raw targets $100 initial stop risk; unchanged news targets $75 per order per asset. Reduced scenario targets $5 per news order per asset; the larger margin-compatible scenario targets $10 per order per asset. Round UP to 0.01 lots as in the current system. This is not a hard realized loss limit. Reference-dollar sizing is deliberate; do not confuse the numbers with independently compounded EA website returns. Raw sizing uses the logged requested entry and original stop, before fill slippage; reported actual risk uses the recorded fill.
- Illustrative global Swing constraints: gold 100 oz/lot and leverage 1:15, silver 5,000 oz/lot and leverage 1:15, minimum/step 0.01 lot. Silver was increased from 1:9 effective 11 May 2026; the notice excludes Australian and US clients. Reserve both pending sides at full gross margin, with no hedge-margin relief. This is a conservative allocation rule, not an exact reproduction of FTMO's pending-order margin implementation. Unchanged scenario allows up to 100% conservative equity margin, reduced scenarios 80%. The $10 news scenario was added after verifying the newer official silver leverage update, not selected by an optimization search.
- Reduced scenario: no new entries after a $200 daily closed loss, and a $200 combined planned-open-risk ceiling. Previously accepted opposite news orders remain eligible. These are hypothetical research guards only, not a deployment or a promise to prevent gap losses.
- Two-Step: $1,000 Phase 1 target, $500 Phase 2 target, four distinct Prague opening days in each phase, flat before transition; reset balance to $10,000. $500 daily equity-loss allowance relative to Prague midnight balance; $9,000 static equity floor. No 1-Step Best Day rule.
- Administrative delay assumption, NOT an FTMO guarantee: 2 business days between evaluations, 5 business days to funded activation. No artificial trades to satisfy minimum days. First reward request at least 14 calendar days after first funded trade, flat, at least $25 positive gross account profit; initial 80% reward split. Receipt modeled 4 business days after request. No challenge fee refund, taxes or payment-provider fees included.
- Equity checking is a proxy, not a native FTMO equity audit: interior full-minute adverse Gold prices and interior one-second news bid/ask extrema; entry/exit boundary seconds use recorded deal endpoints. Gold minute spread is not a tick-level historical spread series. Different assets' adverse marks can be asynchronous/conservative. Actual equity breaches and executions remain uncertain, even if this proxy detects none. One raw trade on 7 September remains open through Prague midnight because the source broker delayed its session-close exit; daily anchors still reset, but a different broker's holiday schedule could change that exit.
- Additional cost stress: use the separate 250ms native news run and subtract one extra adverse price allowance at entry and again at exit: gold $0.50 each side, silver $0.02 each side; raw gold $0.10 each side. Keep recorded costs, with round-trip commissions at least $7/lot XAU and $47.50/lot XAG. These are disclosed conservative cost scenarios, NOT verified historical FTMO tariffs or a liquidity simulation. Swaps are zero in the selected intraday source trades.
- Resampling: 1,000 joint weekly-block bootstrap paths, seed 20260919, preserving all three strategies, same-event legs and within-week timing. Nine observed weeks / six news releases are a tiny dependent sample. This samples historical patterns; it is NOT a calibrated probability of future funding or payout. Event settings were fitted on the same year, including this window. Contract review/strategy approval is not probabilistically modeled.
- FTMO Swing allows news, but FTMO separately prohibits news-gap exploitation and unrealistic execution practices. This exact pre-news straddle design requires written confirmation from FTMO before treating any numerical result as eligibility for funding or reward.
