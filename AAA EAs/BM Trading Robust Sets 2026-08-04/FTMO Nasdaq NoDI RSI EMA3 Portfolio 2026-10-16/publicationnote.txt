PUBLICATION NOTE — ACCOUNT-NEUTRAL RESEARCH RUNNER TEMPLATE

run.py is the original frozen, account-bound research launcher. It remains
private and unchanged locally under the repository's existing privacy policy.
Its recorded SHA-256 in the historical evidence refers to that original file.

run.public.py is a NEW publication template, not the historical frozen runner.
The only operational change is replacing the embedded tester login with the
required CALYX_RESEARCH_MT5_LOGIN environment input, validated before a case
creates artifacts. No private login or default account identifier is provided.
The template's research engine, preregistered cases, native settings, safety
checks and recorded-result logic are otherwise retained.

No account was queried and no tester was run while preparing this template.
It is for an authorized ISOLATED research tester, never a live/funded account.
The existing local research terminal/helper tree is still required; this is
not a standalone installer and does not supply credentials or terminal setup.

The historical FROZEN.json and other manifests were NOT rewritten. They still
check the private original and are not certification of this public template.
Do not overwrite run.py or bypass its recorded freeze checks. Reproduction
requires the authorized original research environment, or a separately planned
and frozen new study which explicitly records the public template/configuration.

Public reports and ledgers remain the record of the completed historical test;
publishing this template does not rerun, amend or extend those results.
