# QuantLab-style Gold Trio — raw reproduction, 3 years (2026-09-30)

Research only: isolated tester `_Backtests/MT5-DMC-20260811`, no live terminal, no optimisation, nothing installed.
Rules were frozen in `RULES.md` before any test (sources and every "(ours)" assumption listed there).

**Setup.** XAUUSD, Exness-MT5Trial16, $10,000, native MT5 Model 4, 150 ms delay, broker spread/commission/swap,
2023-09-29 → 2026-09-29 after a 90-day warm-up, 1% equity risk per module (lots rounded up), modules independent and
allowed to overlap. Broker real ticks start 2026-01-01; earlier ticks are generated by MT5.

**What was reproduced.** QuantLab does not publish the video version's rules. Its website report documents momentum
(H1), EMA10 trend (H1) and Donchian breakout + volatility (M30). The video version swaps the EMA10 module for "turn of
month" and says "Q4 breakout"; that is tested both ways (all year = B, Oct–Dec only = B-Q4). Turn of month uses the
gold-ETF calendar result (long Day −1 → end of Day +2).

**Metric definitions.**
- **Trades/month** = trades ÷ (days ÷ 30.44). **Trades/day** = trades ÷ weekdays.
- **Consistency** = share of calendar months with a positive net result.
- **Sharpe** = the site definition: daily closed-trade returns, every calendar day counted, annualised with √365.
- **Balance DD** = the closed-trade balance drawdown. **Equity DD** = the native report's relative equity DD, which includes floating P/L and exists for the whole run only.
- **Streaks** are shown as average win / average loss (longest win / longest loss).

**Execution notes (disclosed, not hidden):**
- No entry failures, stop-outs, invalid volumes or invalid stops in any run.
- **Trailing-stop timing (A and B).** Trail updates on the 20:00 bar were rejected at the 21:00 daily break ("market closed", code 10018): A had 7 events, B 1, the video system 8. Each is logged twice. The stop moved on the next bar instead, so a few trails were one bar late.
- **Missed reversals (D).** 38 reversals fell on the 21:00 break, so the old position stayed open until the next signal.
- **Turn of month.** March 2024 was skipped because its last trading day was Good Friday; holidays are not modelled.

These are rule-timing effects, not fills at impossible prices. They are a production fix item (retry after the reopen).

## Findings

1. **The video's numbers were not reproduced at the stated quality.**
   - QuantLab claims PF 1.3 and 15% DD over 2018–2026, 1,252 trades.
   - Our A+B+C reconstruction gives PF 1.16 and 27.4% equity DD over 3 years at 1% risk.
   - At their 0.6% risk the DD would scale to roughly 17%, but the PF does not change.
   - Their exact rules, broker, costs and the 2018–2023 years are unknown to us.
2. **Almost all of the edge is the M30 Donchian breakout.**
   - Inside the video system B made +68.6%, A +20.9% and C +5.5%.
   - B beats its random-direction control by a wide margin, and it held up in the last 12 months (+23.5%, PF 1.50).
3. **Momentum is weak.**
   - PF 1.08, and it lost 9% over the last year.
   - Its short side lost money (−$3.6k) while longs made +$6.3k.
   - Long-only would be an optimisation choice, not the raw rule.
4. **Turn of month shows no calendar edge in these 3 years.**
   - An identical three-session hold in mid-month did better (PF 1.74 vs 1.37).
   - It lost in the last year.
5. **"Q4 only" helps PF, but the sample is small.**
   - B-Q4's PF 2.05 comes from only 66 trades in three seasons.
   - Treat it as a season filter to test inside B's optimisation, not a separate strategy.
6. **QuantLab's website mix (with EMA10) is worse.**
   - 3,730 trades, a 43.8% drawdown and PF 1.06.
   - The EMA10 module alone has a 19-trade longest losing streak.

## Recommendation for optimisation (not started — needs the user's go-ahead)

- **Survivor:** B Donchian breakout + volatility (M30), with "Q4 / season" as one of its filter dimensions.
- **First step:** run B and B-Q4 raw on **5 years** plus 6m/1y confirmations, as PIPELINE.md stage 3–4 requires.
- **Then:** the full stage-5 search (timeframe, entry, stop/trail, 0.5–6R, sessions, direction, filters) with a development/validation/holdout split.
- **Monte Carlo** is mandatory on the best version before any promotion.
- **Not carried forward:** A momentum and C turn of month (failed the raw gate); D (reference only).
  - Momentum long-only could be re-screened only as a new, explicitly counted hypothesis.
