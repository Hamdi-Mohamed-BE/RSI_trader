VIX spike / confirmed reversal — frozen raw daily ETF screen, 2026-10-02

The source video only names VIX mean reversion; every numerical rule below is ours,
not a replication of a disclosed institutional strategy. One configuration only.
No optimisation, active-terminal access, deployment, paid data, or Git push.

Instrument: LONG SVXY, cash funded, no leverage or short borrowing. Signal: VIX.
SVXY is -0.5x DAILY short-term VIX FUTURES exposure, not -0.5x spot VIX.
ETF management/roll economics are embedded in its prices. Trading costs are not
known from daily bars; headline results are gross before broker execution costs.
Daily bars cannot provide MT5 tick-equity DD or FTMO/payout forecasts.

Data: daily SVXY from the market-data connector. Official Cboe VIX daily OHLC is
preferred signal source, with connector VIX used for independent close checks.
Save and hash all source files. End-exclusive 2026-10-02; ignore incomplete today.
Independently reset $10,000 accounts for 6m,1y,3y,5y and annual diagnostic windows.
No inception position; warm indicators and arm state with pre-window observations.
Rules are frozen before examining any strategy results; history has been used in
previous research, so these are not pristine out-of-sample windows.

Spike: VIX close >=25 AND >=1.25 * mean of PREVIOUS 20 session closes.
On the first spike of an episode (prior session did not satisfy spike), arm for
the next 5 VIX/ETF joint sessions. Do not renew the timer on subsequent spikes.
Confirmation within that window: VIX close below prior session LOW and below
prior close. No same-spike-day entry. Consume the arm on confirmation; one entry
per episode, no pyramiding. Signals that occur while a trade is open are not used.
Entry: next joint-session SVXY OPEN after the completed VIX confirmation.
Stop: entry fill minus 2 * prior-session SVXY Wilder ATR(14).
Target: entry fill plus 2 * stop distance (2R). No breakeven or trailing stop.
ATR initialises with the first 14 true ranges, then Wilder recursive smoothing.
Volatility exit: after VIX CLOSE <= its 20-session pre-confirmation mean (frozen
at confirmation), request ETF exit at the NEXT session open. Time exit: request
after 10 held daily bars, fill at the following open. Gap stop takes priority;
pending time/mean exits precede intraday brackets. A gap above TP fills at TP,
not a windfall better price. If both daily SL/TP touched, conservative SL first.
Final open position is marked at last close, not silently forced into a trade.

Sizing: floor whole shares to 1% current closed balance / stop distance, then
cap by available cash. Requested risk is a budget, not a guarantee: gaps can
lose more. Skip <1 share. No minimum-lot override, margin, interest or taxes.
Daily close and adverse intraday sampled equity DD reported separately. Daily
OHLC cannot reconstruct an exact multi-tick path; intraday-DD metric is a bound
from a conservative assumed low-before-high path, not native equity DD.
Annualised Sharpe: close-to-close account returns, sqrt(252), risk-free zero.
Closed-trade PF, WR, max/average streaks; trades/month and session frequency.

Control frozen: 1,000 long-SVXY random-entry schedules per 3y/5y window, exactly
same closed trade count (or flag count mismatch), same entry weekdays, same
max eligible holding horizon sequence as observed baseline realised durations,
same cash/risk/ATR/brackets. Candidate dates permuted chronologically, enforce
non-overlap for the whole allocated horizon. This is an exposure/holding-horizon
matched timing diagnostic, not an exact alternative VIX strategy. Also show
cash-funded buy-and-hold benchmark, not treated as same risk.

Gate: BOTH 3y/5y positive return, PF >=1.15, >=30 closed trades, and return
above 95th percentile of matched random control. Native executable-instrument
ticks, measured incremental costs, statistical evidence, and holdout required
before any production promotion. If raw gate fails stop; no tuning a loser.
Optional diagnostic bootstrap: 10,000 circular block-5 risk-unit paths and
return/PF lower bounds; does NOT produce future pass/payout probabilities.
Illustrative 5bps and 10bps each side are sensitivity scenarios ONLY, not
measured execution costs, not canonical measured-cost gate satisfaction.
Only this single raw configuration is tested; do not select a winning variant.
