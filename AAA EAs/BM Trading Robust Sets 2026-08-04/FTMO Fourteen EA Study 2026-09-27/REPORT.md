# FTMO $10K Swing — your fourteen EAs together

Completed research comparison, September 27, 2026. No live trades, account changes, production settings, BATs, website edits or Git pushes.

## What these results mean

**These are conditional fitted-history scenarios, not reliable real-world pass probabilities.** Fresh native Exness MT5 signal histories are repriced and admitted into ONE shared $10,000 account. They are not native FTMO execution tests. Historical equity is not available tick by tick: open trades are marked using full-stop loss reserves. “Reserve failure” means that approximation crossed a limit; it does not establish an actual historical FTMO breach.

All fourteen requested EAs are enabled in each case. Ordinary trades use fixed $50 or $70 planned stop risk (0.5%/0.7% of INITIAL capital); Gold News uses $30 per side, both pending sides retained. Strict lot rounding down; no adaptive multiplier, martingale, new entry logic or optimization. This is a research overlay, not the current production risk policy.

Gold news has a $2 stop distance, so planned sizing is 0.15 lot per side, not 0.02. Its assumed 0.01 minimum lot fits the cap; the fallback is therefore not invoked. Slippage can increase actual fill-to-stop risk above $30. Commission, swaps and stop gaps can add further losses.

The largest news fill-to-original-stop exposure in the source ledger, resized to 0.15 lot, was $118.14 on 2026-02-11. This is NOT a realized loss and NOT a guaranteed maximum. The simulation uses the larger actual fill exposure for its news stop reserve. At gold $4,000, reserving both 0.15-lot orders at 1:15 consumes about $8,000 margin before other positions. Therefore a $30 news risk target does not mean every event can be admitted.

Period: 2025-09-27 through 2026-09-27 exclusive. Native Model 4, 150 ms simulated delay. All native reports and initial order stops are audited. Tick-coverage quality is shown below; missing real history is generated by MT5.

Two management cases: **margin-only** = no added portfolio gates, still shared modeled margin and hard-rule checks; **guarded** = $300 daily reserved-loss budget, $225 simultaneous initial risk, $150 per symbol/correlated metals, projected equity above $9,200, seven entries/day, stop new entries after three net losses, at most 80% reserved margin. Both news pending sides consume resources. These gates reject signals; they are not guaranteed loss caps and do not liquidate existing trades.

Current FTMO public instrument API confirms XAU and US100 Swing leverage 1:15; USDJPY 1:30. Both news sides are reserved with no hedge-margin relief. Lot step/minimum 0.01 remains a platform-specification assumption. Native spread/gap prices and swaps are reused, not historical FTMO quotes. Commission floors and adverse execution/carry sensitivities are in PROTOCOL.md.

FTMO model: +$1,000 Challenge then +$500 Verification, four opening days per phase, flat at passage; Prague midnight balance minus $500 daily equity floor; $9,000 static total equity floor. No 2-Step Best Day Rule and no evaluation expiry. Two business days between phases and five to funded activation are assumptions. First reward requested when flat, at least fourteen calendar days after first funded entry, with $25+ closed profit; 80% share; assumed receipt four business days later. Simulation stops at first request. Actual approval, payment and longer funded-account survival are not modeled.

## Decision limits

- No automatic deployment: this is a study, not a verified live passing plan.
- FTMO Swing news permission does not confirm approval of this particular pre-release two-sided straddle. Gap-trading and cumulative-risk restrictions still apply. Obtain written eligibility clarification.
- Joint FTMO bid/ask equity, historical spreads/swaps, lot minima, rejection/fill behavior, server request counts and forward results remain to be verified. Model-implied payout is not promised payment.
- Do not interpret a high win rate as safety: payoff size, shared exposure and adverse execution determine account survival.
