Gann-inspired range boxes, fans and time cycles

Gold (XAUUSD), US100 (Exness USTEC), Bitcoin (BTCUSD). Four tradable modes plus three controls per asset. $10,000 independent accounts, 0.5% current-balance nominal stop risk. Long and short. No optimisation or live deployment.

Complete: 42 / 42 native cases. No failed cases.

These are mechanical adaptations, NOT an exact original Gann strategy. Pre-2026 history uses generated ticks from broker bars; three-year native quality is typically 25% real ticks. Fresh 2026 cases are verified separately at 100%. The last two years were not used to fit or select settings in this screen, but these markets/dates were previously explored in other research: not globally untouched OOS.

Frozen rules, clocks and assumptions
  • reference: Last completed broker-UTC D1 range for gold/US100; last completed Monday-UTC W1 range for BTC. Frozen until next reference period.
  • rejection: Bullish close inside lower eighth after touching its boundary: long to midpoint, stop below min(previous range low, signal low)-0.1 ATR. Mirror shorts in upper eighth. Quartile control changes only fraction from 1/8 to 1/4.
  • range breakout: Completed close crosses prior range high/low; require a later directional candle to retest boundary within eight signal bars and close outside. Stop beyond boundary/retest wick by 0.25 ATR; target 2R.
  • fan: Confirmed H4 body-independent high/low pivots: strict two completed neighbours on each side. Ascending fan from a higher low, descending from lower high. Slope frozen from price change between latest two confirmed same-type pivots divided by their tradable H4-bar separation (at least four). One 1x1 slope tested, maximum age 32 H4 bars; completed break followed by retest within eight signal bars. Stop wick/line +0.25 ATR; target 2R.
  • cycles: H1 EMA12/26 crossover; stop 1.5 ATR14, target 2R. Gate permits signals at H1 trading-bar ages 8/16/32 +/-1 after latest H4 pivot CONFIRMATION, not its hindsight timestamp. New pivot replaces clock. Baseline has no gate.
  • random control: Seed 26100950 and pivot confirmation timestamp generate three separated centres in 2..32, +/-1-bar windows: same nine of 33 eligible ages as cycle gate. Fixed before outcomes; one native placebo, not proof from a single random comparison.
  • execution: Next tick after completed signal; at most one successful trade per UTC day and one position per independent account. No additional session/news/ADX/DI filters; BTC weekends included. Entry through 20:30 UTC, close at first tick >=20:55 UTC or after eight hours. No trailing/partials.
  • risk: 0.5% current closed balance; volume floored to broker step. Skip below-minimum lots, invalid price geometry or insufficient margin. Cash risk is nominal, not guaranteed maximum loss.

Gold uses M15, US100 M5, BTC M15 for box/fan signals; all cycle/control EMA entries use H1. ATR readings come only from the just-completed entry-timeframe candle. H4 pivot confirmation needs two later completed bars; entry is never backdated to the swing.

Gann documentation describes configurable price/time partitions and explicit scaling, not evidence that these defaults are profitable: TradingView price/time scale reference · Gann box reference.
