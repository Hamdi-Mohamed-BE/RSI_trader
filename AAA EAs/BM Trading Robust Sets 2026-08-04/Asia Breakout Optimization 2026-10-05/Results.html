What the code audit found

The visible InpTrailStartR/InpTrailDistanceR inputs do not affect Asia’s hardcoded manager. New stable management keeps a fixed TP-implied R=(originalTP−filled entry)/configuredRR; it does not recompute R from the moved SL. Small fill differences mean this is not identical to original entry-to-SL risk.

Clock mismatch: current historical tests use mode1 (UTC+2/+3 assumption). Exness live automatic offset is UTC0.
