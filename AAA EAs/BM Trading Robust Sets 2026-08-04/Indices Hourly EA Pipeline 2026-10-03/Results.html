Why the original hourly comparison differs

The original table compared independent hourly cells on $1m execution capital and excluded incomplete or delayed holds afterwards. The universal EA uses all selected hours together on $10k, with one owned position at a time, known-session checks, actual delayed exits and no post-execution exclusions. Native windows start at midnight UTC, so the first evening trade may have the previous NY date. The complete ledgers and native report profits reconcile, including magic-zero broker-forced stop-outs recovered from native reports.
