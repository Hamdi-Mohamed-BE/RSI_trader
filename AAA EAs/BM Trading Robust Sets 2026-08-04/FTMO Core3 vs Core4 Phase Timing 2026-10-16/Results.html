What this replay does—and does not measure

  • Phase 1 needs $11,000; Phase 2 restarts at $10,000 and needs $10,500. Each phase requires four distinct Prague entry days and no active positions at its natural pass event. Official FTMO trading objectives.
  • Only weekday start dates with a full 180-day follow-up are included. Holidays are not removed. Pending or waiting accounts at the horizon are not counted as failed.
  • Phase 2’s conditional denominator includes every account activated before the horizon, even if it has no trades. An unstarted Phase 2 is not put in that denominator.
  • Current sizing is retained as a source-request cap plus a maximum $50 protected initial-stop budget, floored to broker lot steps. Too-small orders are rejected. This is not a guaranteed realized-loss ceiling.
  • The entry model includes $225 aggregate/$150 per-symbol initial risk, $300 persistent daily entry stop, $9,200 admission buffer, daily entry/loss limits and a margin proxy. It is not a complete native FTMO implementation.
  • Equity is reconstructed from causal, uniquely owned five-minute source marks. Initial floating is zero until the first interior mark; two source positions have no interior mark. Past marks can remain stale through market closures. Missing intraminute excursions can change breach and admission outcomes.
  • The engine retains historical exits and the filled source opportunity stream. It cannot recreate counterfactual signals after a skipped trade, complete live stop-management/request state, actual broker margin or all open-swap reserves.
  • Recorded costs are Exness proxies, including zero-spread archives—not certified FTMO execution. Cost stress adds per-side USTEC 1 point, USDJPY 0.01 JPY and XAUUSD $0.10, and doubles negative recorded swap; exits are not quote-reexecuted.
  • The five-year source reports say 15% real ticks, with real ticks beginning 1 January 2026. Earlier parts of this latest matching year contain generated ticks. The declared 2026-start subgroup is displayed; it is not newly selected optimisation.
  • The model conservatively treats equality to a hard floor as a breach, unlike strictly-below wording. The 180-day horizon is analytical, not an FTMO deadline. Review and access arithmetic are assumptions, not firm guarantees; see FTMO’s review and qualification FAQ.

Verification and evidence

Passing the audit confirms source ownership, model implementation, cash arithmetic and statistical denominators—not a validated future edge or calibrated pass probability.

Frozen protocol limitations
    No EA, BAT, live account or website was changed. Do not deploy a basket solely because these selected-history replay fractions are high.
