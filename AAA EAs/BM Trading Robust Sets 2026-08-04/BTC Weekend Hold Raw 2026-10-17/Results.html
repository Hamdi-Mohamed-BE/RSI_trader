Definitions and limitations

  • Entry ask proxy = scheduled bid bar open + its recorded spread points × $0.01. Exit = scheduled bid bar open. A bar's spread is not a timestamp-matched opening ask quote; this is not a tick-fill backtest.
  • PF = sum positive completed-trade P&L ÷ absolute sum negative P&L; undefined if there is no loss. Win rate uses positive P&L / all completed trades. A zero resets both streaks.
  • Closed DD uses the chronological closed-cash balance, including the starting balance. Sampled equity DD uses every available M1 bid OPEN while held (M5 in the long panel), plus initial cash; it is not true tick or intrabar equity DD. USD and percentage maxima are independent maxima.
  • Daily cash Sharpe uses all UTC calendar dates, including zero-trade days: mean(realized daily P&L / prior closed balance) ÷ sample standard deviation × √365. It excludes unrealized daily returns and is not an annual-return forecast.
  • Fractional BTC is a research reference: broker lot minimums, lot steps, leverage and margin, liquidity, slippage, commissions and historical financing are not verified. No profit target, risk optimization, live installation or order execution.
  • One weekend rule tested as specified, with no timing search. The longer record is descriptive, not untouched proof; overlapping recent windows are not independent tests.
