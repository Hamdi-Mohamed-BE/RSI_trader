No live installation, account mutation, website/BAT change, paid data or optimisation.

PROFILE
Same daily New York anchor for all assets: 09:30 inclusive to 10:30 exclusive.
USTEC/XAU weekdays only; BTC also weekends. US daylight-saving rules, broker UTC.
Require all 60 completed M1 bars, timestamps and positive tick volume; otherwise skip.
64 equal-width price bins between initial-hour low/high. Allocate each M1 tick-volume
count uniformly over its high-low interval, weighted by price-bin overlap; zero-range
bar goes into its price bin. This is an OHLC tick-volume approximation, NOT actual
exchange volume-at-price and NOT individual broker quote locations.
POC is largest bin, first/lower on ties. Grow contiguous value area from POC towards
the larger adjacent bin until at least 70% of volume; grow upwards on equal volumes.
Freeze the initial-hour POC/VAL/VAH for the rest of that day; never use full-day future data.
Classify with normalized POC p, volume centroid c and (last close-first open)/range d:
P: p >= .65 AND c >= .60 AND d >= .25.
b: p <= .35 AND c <= .40 AND d <= -.25.
D: .35 <= p <= .65 AND .40 <= c <= .60 AND abs(d) <= .25.
Other/unclassified: no entries. Labels describe our heuristic, not observed buyer/seller aggression.

SIGNALS (completed M5 candles after profile ends)
ATR14 is arithmetic mean of true ranges of the 14 M5 candles BEFORE the signal candle.
Conviction comparison is signal tick volume versus mean of preceding 20 M5 tick volumes.
1 RECLAIM: P buys a bullish candle that sweeps below VAL and closes strictly inside VA;
b sells bearish sweep above VAH closing inside. D permits either mirror fade. Target
opposite VA edge. Stop beyond signal extreme by .1 prior ATR. Candle wicks suffice;
no multi-bar excursion memory in this baseline. Reject simultaneous buy/sell ambiguity.
2 WEAK FADE: on P, bearish wick above VAH closing inside, volume <= prior20 mean,
sell towards POC; on b, bullish wick below VAL closing inside, same volume condition,
buy towards POC. Stop beyond wick by .1 ATR. D excluded to avoid duplicate reclaim.
3 STRONG BREAKOUT: previous M5 close inside inclusive VA, then P long/b short (D either)
closes > VAH+.1 ATR / < VAL-.1 ATR, direction agrees with candle colour, directional
body >= .5 ATR and volume >= 1.5 x prior20 mean. Stop at opposite VA edge minus/plus
.1 ATR. Fixed 2R target from next-tick quote. No assumed retest, delta, trailing or BE.
4 COMBINED: identical modules evaluated reclaim then weak-fade then strong-breakout.
One open position per asset/test. At most one eligible attempt per module per NY day.
Used flag set only once basic stop/target/spread/lot/session checks pass; failed order
does consume attempt. Combined can trade up to three non-overlapping trades/day.
No entries before 10:35 or at/after 15:30 NY. Request flat at 16:00 or 10 minutes
before broker session close; retry once/minute while tradable. Export carryovers.
