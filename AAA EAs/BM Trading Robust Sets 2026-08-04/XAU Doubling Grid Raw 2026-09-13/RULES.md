# XAU doubling grid — raw, no optimization

Scope: gold only; tester-only EA; no live trades, installers or website changes. User explicitly removed BTC from scope.

- Independent $3,000 USD starting accounts for 3y (first), 6m, 1y and 5y. Preserve the existing system windows ending 2026-09-05 exclusive. A period label is a requested horizon, NOT survival to that date.
- Enter a 0.02-lot buy at the first available tradable quote. No indicator or session filter.
- "$10" means a $10 move in XAUUSD quoted price, not $10 account P&L. Grid spacing is anchored to the first actual entry price: add 0.04 lots at first entry minus $10, then 0.08 at minus $20, 0.16 at minus $30, etc. Trigger buys with the executable ask, not bid. Fill at available prices, not idealized historical rung prices; at most one add per incoming tick.
- With one leg: close when executable bid reaches first entry plus $10. With two or more legs: close the ENTIRE basket when executable bid reaches the first entry price. First leg is gross price breakeven, not net breakeven after fees. Sequential market closes can slip; no guaranteed fill price.
- After a basket closes, immediately start the next basket on the next tick unless today's net cash P&L is at least $300. Daily net cash includes trading commissions, swaps and realized profit. Reset at broker calendar midnight. Open baskets can carry overnight/weekends and are not liquidated merely because the date changes. Daily target is checked only when flat, so the full basket may overshoot it.
- No voluntary stop loss, no equity stop, no arbitrary maximum number of rungs, no risk optimization and no withdrawal/top-up. Broker lot limits, margin availability and stop-out are enforced. If a prescribed doubled lot is unaffordable, do not replace it with a smaller lot or invent a fill: record the failure, keep the existing basket, and retry the same rung (no faster than once per minute) while still below its trigger. A broker maximum-lot limit is recorded separately.
- Failure endpoint: FIRST broker stop-out/forced-liquidation deal. Stop that simulation and do not recapitalize/restart it. If MT5 itself ends the test on stop-out, use its report/journal and the full deal ledger. Any residual test-end liquidation is labelled as such, not a new strategy basket win/loss.
- Success milestone: a CLOSED, FLAT account balance of $6,000 or more before the first broker stop-out ($3,000 net profit). Also report the first floating-equity crossing of $6,000 separately; floating profit is not secured profit. Continue after a milestone until requested end or first stop-out to test whether gains survive. Profits are retained, not withdrawn.
- Native MT5 model 4, broker Exness-MT5Trial16 / XAUUSD Zero, current account leverage 1:2000, margin-call 30%, stop-out 0%, hedging. Verify these INSIDE the tester. Spread, commission and swap use the loaded native broker specifications; historical fee/leverage regime changes are not reconstructed. Fixed 1ms execution delay is not a worst-case slippage model.
- Real ticks in this history begin 2026-01-01; older history can be generated. Label actual coverage and early termination honestly. Do not claim this single start per window estimates a probability of survival or a guaranteed income.

Evidence: native reports, transaction ledger with stop-out reasons, grid decisions, minute equity samples, per-tick equity extrema, daily balance records and the $6,000 milestones. Independent checks must reconcile cash P&L/costs, entry sizes/rungs, basket exits, daily cutoff and the ordering of profit milestone versus stop-out.
