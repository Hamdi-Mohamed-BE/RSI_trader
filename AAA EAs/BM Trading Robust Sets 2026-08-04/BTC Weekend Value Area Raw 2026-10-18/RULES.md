# BTCUSD weekend value-area limits — raw rules (frozen 2026-10-18, before any test)

Source: user idea + TradingView FRVP screenshots (2026-10-18). Research only, isolated tester, nothing deployed.

## User rules
- After each **Friday** day ends, build that day's volume profile.
- FRVP settings from the screenshots:
  - Rows Layout "Number of rows", **Row size 128**.
  - **Value Area Volume 80%**.
  - Volume "Up/Down", which only changes the colouring; the value area uses total volume.
- During the weekend, place a **buy limit at VAL** and a **sell limit at VAH**.
- **Target the other side** (buy → VAH, sell → VAL) with **1:3 RR**, so the stop is 1/3 of the VA width beyond the entry:
  - buy SL = VAL − (VAH−VAL)/3
  - sell SL = VAH + (VAH−VAL)/3

## Our defaults (fill the gaps; fixed before testing)
- **Day / clock:** Friday = 00:00–24:00 UTC. Exness server time is UTC. The weekend = Saturday 00:00 → Monday 00:00 UTC. **(ours)**
- **Volume:** MT5 tick volume of Friday's M1 bars. BTCUSD is a CFD, so there is no exchange volume. Each bar's volume is spread evenly over the price rows its high–low range covers. **(ours)**
- **Value area:**
  - 128 equal rows between Friday's high and low.
  - POC = the row with the highest volume.
  - The VA grows from the POC one row at a time, always adding the neighbouring row with more volume, until it holds 80% of the total.
  - VAH = top of the highest VA row; VAL = bottom of the lowest. **(ours; TradingView's exact internal algorithm is not published)**
- **Orders:**
  - Both limits are placed from the first weekend tick.
  - If price is already beyond a level (e.g. below VAL), that order waits until it is valid, i.e. price is back on the correct side.
  - At most **one fill per side per weekend**.
  - **No OCO**: both sides may fill. **(ours)**
- **End of weekend, main version (WVA):** unfilled orders are cancelled at Monday 00:00 UTC, and open positions are closed then. **(ours)**
- **Variant WVAH:** identical, but filled positions are held until TP or SL with no time exit. A side is skipped on a later weekend while its previous position is still open. **(ours, declared up front)**
- **Risk:** 1% of equity per order, lots rounded **up** to the lot step (broker minimum if needed), as with every Calyx EA. Orders are skipped if margin or broker stop-distance checks fail.
- **Control (CRNG):** the same rules, with the levels replaced by Friday's **price-range** 10% and 90% points (no volume). This tests whether the volume profile adds anything over a plain range.

## Test setup
- **Account and execution:** Exness-MT5Trial16 BTCUSD, $10,000, 1:2000, native MT5 Model 4 (every tick based on real ticks), 150 ms delay, broker spread/commission/swap.
- **Tick data:** real ticks start 2026-01-01; earlier ticks are generated by MT5.
- **Windows:** 6m / 1y / 3y / 5y ending 2026-10-13. The last complete weekend is 10–11 Oct.

## Raw gate (PIPELINE.md stage 4)
- Positive with PF ≥ 1.15 on **both 3y and 5y**.
- ≥ 30 trades.
- Better than the control (PF and mean R).
- No execution failures.

FAIL → stop (no optimisation of a loser). PASS or a near miss → report and ask before stage 5.
