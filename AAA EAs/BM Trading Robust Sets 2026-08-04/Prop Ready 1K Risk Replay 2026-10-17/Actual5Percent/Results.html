How to interpret compounding here

Dynamic 5% reduces requested cash risk after losses and raises it after gains, subject to broker lot steps and the stated guards. Fixed $50 does not reduce risk with a shrinking balance: $50 is 6.25% after cash falls to $800. The reference cap is not silently raised in the old profile; this is a separately labelled uncapped experiment.

Differences can also come from admitted trade subsets and entry priority, not simply a multiplier applied to final profits. Rejected trades can change an EA’s future state, generating signals absent from standalone source runs. That missing counterfactual order stream is not recreated here.
