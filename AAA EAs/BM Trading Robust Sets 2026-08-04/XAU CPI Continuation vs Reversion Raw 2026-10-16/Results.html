What is being compared?

Common setup

  • Official documented CPI release dates, 08:30 New York with DST handled; 81 scheduled events. Headline/core CPI are one simultaneous release, not duplicate opportunities.
  • Anchor: exact completed M1 close before release. ATR: simple mean of 14 true ranges using 15 contiguous pre-event M1 bars. All required data end strictly before release.
  • First release-aligned minute only: close displacement ≥1 pre-event ATR and close in the directional outer 25%. This is a price-reaction filter, not actual-versus-consensus surprise.
  • One signal attempt per model/event. Valid fresh quotes, spread ≤10% of stop distance, downward-rounded lot sizing. No forced market fallback, trailing, break-even, or partials.

Frozen entries and exits

  • Continuation: executable break of the first minute’s extreme after its close, within 5 minutes of release. Stop beyond the opposite extreme + 0.1 ATR + one tick; target 1.5R; hold ≤15 minutes from fill.
  • Reversion: a later completed M1 close crosses the first candle’s midpoint against the impulse; next executable quote, within 10 minutes. Stop beyond the spike extreme; target the pre-release anchor; minimum offered reward/risk 1.5R; hold ≤30 minutes.
  • Anchor diagnostic: same reversion, with no minimum-R gate. Predeclared to expose the midpoint/anchor geometry—not optimized or eligible for selection.
  • All modes hard-flat by release +60 minutes, at an available executable quote. Trading is prohibited outside the strategy tester.
The strict anchor fade may take zero trades because returning from the midpoint to the pre-event anchor often offers less than 1.5R with the stop beyond the spike. That is a structural exclusion, not proof that reversals never occur. The continuation/reversion P&L difference is not a pure directional effect: their confirmations, target geometry and holding times differ.

Separate event-study evidence

Unconditional t+1 → t+5/15/30-minute price comparisons are separate from trade P&L and the strategy filters. Continuation means the horizon close moves farther in the first minute’s direction; reversion means the opposite increment—not necessarily a complete return to the pre-news price. A directional outcome is not the probability of a 1.5R trade win.

Accounting and interpretation

Full trade cash is reconciled to native deal cash, tester net profit and final balance. Successful entry decisions are matched to native fills within five seconds and the same scheduled release; rejected entry requests are excluded from matching. Causality checks require entry at or after release +60 seconds and at most one completed trade per event.

Realized net R is net P&L divided by the estimated cash risk from the actual fill to the initial stop, using native request-unit loss and linear price-distance scaling. It is not a guarantee of the maximum realized loss. Lot rounding and delayed fills can make realized risk differ from the 0.25% planned budget. Gold price differences are USD per troy ounce, not MT5 points; native OrderCalcProfit converts actual broker lots to USD.

Wilson intervals describe the observed trade sample only; they are not independent-event bootstrap intervals, proof of stationarity, or correction for previous research. Multiple assets were not tested in this study. No Monte Carlo or optimization result is implied.
