Bitcoin epoch-folding experiment -- frozen before downloading / scoring results
Date: 2026-10-02. Research only. No trading account, orders, EA deployment or Git push.

Instrument: Binance SPOT BTCUSDT, not a broker CFD or futures contract.
Source: official Binance public archive, 1-minute klines, SHA256 checksums.
Test: 2025-10-02 00:00 UTC through 2026-10-02 00:00 UTC, exclusive end (365 days).
Warm-up/training: 2025-04-01 onward; preceding day supplies the first close-to-close return.
Require monotonic unique one-minute timestamps, OHLC validity, full coverage; report missing
bars and do not interpolate returns across gaps. Exclude incomplete 15-minute RV bins.

Signal visualization: epoch-fold absolute one-minute log returns by minute within the hour,
minute of UTC day, and minute of week. No directional trading interpretation.
Primary forecasts: next 15-minute realized variance (sum of squared minute log returns).
Forecast issued before the bin starts; realized return uses closes only after issuance.
Models: EWMA level only; EWMA plus hourly phase (4 bins); daily phase (96 bins);
daily plus weekly phase (672 bins). EWMA lambda 0.97 for all models, not optimized.
Daily refit using trailing 180 days, excluding the forecast day. Seasonality estimated
from RV normalized by each UTC week's in-sample mean to reduce regime-level confounding.
Daily pattern cyclically smoothed with a 3-bin average. Weekly pattern smoothed within
each weekday with 3 bins and shrunk toward the daily profile with 30 pseudo-observations.
Hourly smoothed not applied (4 phase bins). Clip factors to [0.15, 6], normalize to mean 1.
Estimate deseasonalized EWMA level using the previous 7 days and update online after each
realization, multiplying by the calendar factor of the next bin. All models have the same
initialization window and update equation. No parameter chosen using the test results.
QLIKE = realized/forecast - log(realized/forecast) - 1, lower is better.
Actual zero-RV bins are excluded from this dimensionless score and counted explicitly.
Report mean, % reduction vs baseline, 95% paired moving-block bootstrap intervals, and
monthly consistency. Bootstrap daily paired losses, circular blocks 7 days, 5,000 draws,
seed 20261002. Include 14-day-block sensitivity. Holm correction over the three baseline
comparisons and the incremental weekly-vs-daily comparison (4 tests).
Secondary robustness: EWMA lambda 0.94 and 0.99, all models / all results retained.
No strategy P&L, win rate, PF or Sharpe claimed: a volatility predictor is not a trading bot.

Period discovery uses PRE-TEST training only, aggregated mean absolute return in 5-minute
bins. Search 14 predeclared periods in minutes: 30,45,60,90,120,180,240,360,480,720,
1440,2880,4320,10080. Phase-bin width 5 minutes. Statistic is relative variance of folded
mean profile. Use complete UTC Monday-aligned 7-day blocks, normalize each by its mean,
and generate 999 independent circular shifts of each block. This retains within-block
volatility clusters (except one wrap boundary), destroys fixed-clock alignment, and does
NOT assume independent Gaussian noise. Bonferroni-adjust empirical p-values for 14 trials.
Sensitivity: 14-day shift blocks. Periods are aliases/harmonics, not independent cycles.
Discovery is separate from forecast evaluation; no discovered period replaces fixed
hourly/daily/weekly forecast models. Report full grid, including failures.

QA: unit tests for folding, gap handling, causal update alignment, seeded null inference,
synthetic seasonal recovery, forecast-only past-data invariance and checksum integrity.
Output: source manifest, test forecasts/losses, daily/monthly scores, significance table,
static scientific figures and standalone results report. No live settings changed.
