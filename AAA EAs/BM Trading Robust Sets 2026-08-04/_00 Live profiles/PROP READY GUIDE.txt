PROP READY - NINE EAs / BROKER-NEUTRAL DEMO OR LIVE

Start: Prop ready.bat
Dedicated package: Packages/PropReady (NOT the FTMO-only guarded package).

COMPATIBILITY
Use a normal MT5 HEDGING demo or live account with fully tradeable cash gold,
Nasdaq100 and USDJPY symbols. No FTMO-company restriction, no USD-only currency
restriction, no $10,000 balance restriction and no 1:30 leverage restriction.
Aliases/suffixes are resolved at installation; broker margin and minimum lots
still apply. NETTING is rejected: it merges independent EAs' positions.
Missing/unsupported symbols stop installation; no unrelated asset or futures
contract is substituted. Cent accounts use their displayed currency units.

THE SAME NINE EAs
1. Nasdaq M5 Momentum - DI ON by default, trailing unchanged.
2. USDJPY London Momentum - ADX >=20; DI ON by default.
3. US100 Month End Flow.
4. Gold RSI VWAP - current 0.5R version.
5. US100 H1 ORB 13UTC - 0.5R.
6. US100 New York ORB M30 - 0.5R.
7. US100 Selective ORB V3 - 0.5R.
8. Gold New York ORB M30 - 0.5R.
9. Gold London-New York Overlap ORB M30 - 0.5R.
No news or stop-less hourly EAs. Signals, stops and targets are unchanged.

REQUIREMENTS
Windows, running/logged-in MT5, python.exe and its MetaTrader5 module. The
installer is not Python-free; compiled EAs run inside MT5 after installation
without a Python trading service. Retain the whole package/sibling folders.
Flat account, no pending orders, Algo Trading OFF. Use one terminal/copy only;
no manual/VPS trading during installation or portfolio operation.

DEMO TEST
1. Log into your DEMO account and switch Algo Trading OFF.
2. Run Prop ready.bat. Choose percentage or fixed cash, then the amount.
3. Choose Nasdaq DI and USDJPY DI independently; ON remains the default.
4. Review account, currency, resolved symbols, nine EAs and effective limits.
5. Type the exact confirmation. Identity, balance, exposure and permissions
   are checked again before files change.
6. Only the selected terminal is cleanly restarted into a NEW nine-chart
   profile, Algo Trading OFF. Review charts/Inputs, then enable it yourself.
No orders placed by installer. No existing positions automatically closed.

RISK SETTINGS
Fixed cash is ACCOUNT CURRENCY: 50 means USD50 on USD, EUR50 on EUR, etc.
It is NOT an FX-converted USD amount on non-USD accounts. The legacy command
parameter FIXED_USD is retained for compatibility only. Fixed default: 50.
Percentage mode default: 0.5% of current equity.
Additional hard ceiling: 0.5% of saved initial deployment balance per entry,
retaining a smaller original strategy volume where applicable. Lots round DOWN;
if the minimum lot does not fit, no entry. 10K USD -> USD50 cap; 5K -> USD25.
Larger requests cannot bypass this. Gaps, costs, swaps, slippage and outages
can still make actual losses exceed planned initial-stop risk.

GUARDS (SCALED FROM INITIAL DEPLOYMENT BALANCE)
Aggregate original stop risk <=2.25%; per symbol <=1.5%; daily reserved-loss
budget 3%; equity entry buffer 92% of reference. Hard latched floor: 90% of
reference or daily loss 5% of reference. Seven entries / three completed net
losing positions maximum per Prague day. Margin <=80% of protected equity
and within free margin. Original stop risk stays reserved when stops trail.
These block NEW entries, not forced liquidation; position management remains
available after entry stops/history failure. Firm-specific rules can differ.

PERSISTENCE / HISTORY
First flat-account installation saves reference/start in:
MQL5/Files/Calyx-PropReady-<login>-<server hash>.json
Installation-day anchor is that approved balance; later days reconstruct the
day-start from history. Re-running retains reference/start, never clears loss
latches. Do NOT delete/edit this state or terminal variables to bypass a stop.
Post-install deposits/withdrawals/credit adjustments or unavailable history
block new entries until review. Login/server/currency/symbol remain bound.

CONTINUOUS MODE / PHASES
Default internal Phase=Funded means continuous demo/live operation WITHOUT
a challenge profit-target stop; it does not assert an actual funded account.
Optional -Phase Challenge or -Phase Verification applies a relative 10% or
5% profit admission stop after four trading days. These optional screens do
not declare any firm's current rules. The separate FTMO guarded BAT remains
FTMO USD10K Swing restricted, with its original guard/settings intact.

OFFLINE FILE CHECK (no account access or installation):
  & ".\Prop ready.bat" -ValidateOnly

READ-ONLY COMPATIBILITY CHECK (reads selected logged-in MT5 account):
  & ".\Prop ready.bat" -PreflightOnly
Follow risk/DI prompts; no installation/restart in this mode.

HISTORY CAVEAT
Original $10K/fixed-$50/DI-ON FTMO screening is NOT a backtest of a different
broker, currency, size or custom risk/DI setting. No future profit, pass rate
or identical fills are promised. Demo-test first. Building these files does
not deploy to MT5, update the website or push to GitHub.
