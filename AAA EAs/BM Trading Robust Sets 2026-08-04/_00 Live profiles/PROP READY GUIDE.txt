PROP READY — NINE-EA GUARDED LAUNCHER

Start: Prop ready.bat
Profile selection: prop-ready / PROP_READY9

WHAT IT IS
This is the nine-EA research shortlist, packaged through the existing FTMO
guarded installer. It is intended ONLY for a USD $10,000 FTMO 2-Step Swing
hedging account. The name does not mean compatibility with every prop firm.
MT5 account details cannot prove which product you purchased; verify it yourself.
Nothing is installed, traded or enabled merely by receiving these files.

THE EXACT NINE EAs
1. Nasdaq M5 Momentum — DI ON by default.
2. USDJPY London Momentum — ADX >=20; DI ON by default.
3. US100 Month End Flow.
4. Gold RSI VWAP — current 0.5R version.
5. US100 H1 ORB 13UTC — 0.5R.
6. US100 New York ORB M30 — 0.5R.
7. US100 Selective ORB V3 — 0.5R.
8. Gold New York ORB M30 — 0.5R.
9. Gold London–New York Overlap ORB M30 — 0.5R.
No news or stop-less hourly strategies are included.

REQUIREMENTS
- Windows with MT5 already running and logged into the intended account.
- Python available as python.exe, with the MetaTrader5 Python module installed.
  Actual installation/account preflight requires these; this launcher does not
  install them. It is NOT a Python-free installer.
- Keep the BAT in its original folder and retain the supplied package tree,
  including Start-LiveProfile.ps1, Profiles.json, Packages/FTMO, and the shared
  _Auto Deploy installer helpers. Copying the BAT alone is not sufficient.
- A flat account: no open positions and no pending orders. Algo Trading OFF.
  Use one terminal/copy only; disable other copies/VPS trading on this account.

RUNNING IT
1. Check the account, product and phase in MT5. Turn Algo Trading OFF. Do not
   place trades or let another copy trade while the installer is waiting.
2. Double-click Prop ready.bat. Choose fixed USD or percentage allocation,
   enter its value, and choose DI ON/OFF for Nasdaq and USDJPY separately.
3. Recommended/tested-model defaults: FIXED USD 50, both DI filters ON.
   Percentage mode offers 0.5% by default, but is a custom allocation choice,
   not necessarily equivalent to fixed $50 as equity and lot steps change.
4. Review the selected terminal, account, nine EAs and settings. Type the
   installer’s exact account/phase confirmation only if they are correct.
5. After confirmation the selected terminal is cleanly closed and restarted
   into a newly named profile. Algo Trading remains OFF. No existing position
   is automatically closed to make installation possible.
6. Review all NINE charts, symbols, Inputs, account and phase. Enable Algo
   Trading yourself only after you are satisfied. Keep monitoring the account.

RISK AND GUARD LIMITS
The chosen fixed amount or percentage is a REQUESTED allocation ceiling.
The existing guard retains the source-request volume cap and a hard maximum
of $50 PLANNED initial-stop risk per entry, rounding lots DOWN. Requests above
$50 cannot raise that ceiling. Too-small allocations may produce no valid lot.
Slippage, gaps, fees, swaps and failures can still make actual losses larger.

Existing guard policies remain: $225 aggregate / $150 per-symbol initial-stop
risk, $300 daily entry-stop protection, a $9,200 admission buffer, at most seven
entries per Prague day, no new entries after three completed net losing
positions that day, and a margin cap. Hard account floors are $9,000 equity
and Prague day-start balance minus $500. These are ENTRY guards, not forced
liquidation or guaranteed compliance.
Existing safety latches are not reset by giving the profile a new name.

PHASE / ACCOUNT CHANGES
Default phase: Challenge. A new phase or account requires running the launcher
again; it does not switch phases/accounts automatically. From PowerShell in
this folder, use the appropriate explicit phase when needed:

  & ".\Prop ready.bat" -Phase Verification
  & ".\Prop ready.bat" -Phase Funded

CHECKS WITHOUT INSTALLING
  & ".\Prop ready.bat" -ValidateOnly
    File/settings/integrity checks only. No account access or MT5 restart.
    Passing this does not prove broker/account compatibility.

  & ".\Prop ready.bat" -PreflightOnly
    Read-only checks of the selected running MT5/account, including the Python
    probe; nothing is installed or restarted. Follow the displayed prompts.

IF A CHECK FAILS
Stop and read the error; do not bypass it or weaken the guard. Missing Python
or MetaTrader5 dependencies, wrong product/account, open trades/orders, Algo
Trading ON, unsupported symbols or altered package files require correction
before continuing. No force-kill or automatic position cleanup is intended.

WHAT THE RESEARCH DOES NOT PROMISE
The nine-EA shortlist was selected from 12 predeclared historical baskets using
overlapping starts and sampled five-minute floating equity, not genuine FTMO
execution or untouched forward data. A >=75% historical gate — or even 134/134
historical completions — is NOT a future pass-probability guarantee.
The model’s fixed-$50 / DI-ON results do not describe custom risk, percentage
mode, either DI filter OFF, another account size, broker or prop-firm programme.
Actual account review/KYC, access dates, costs and live outcomes can differ.

This launcher does not update the website, deploy to another device or push Git
changes. Keep the original risk warnings and review the research before use.
