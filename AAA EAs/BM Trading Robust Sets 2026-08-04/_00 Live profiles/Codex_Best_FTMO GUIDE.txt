BROKER COMPATIBILITY
There is no FTMO-only, USD-only, leverage or $10,000 balance lock. Demo and live
MT5 HEDGING accounts are supported, subject to the broker providing tradeable
cash CFDs for Nasdaq and gold plus USDJPY and valid contract/lot rules. Netting
and exchange-netting accounts are deliberately blocked: they would merge the
independent bots' positions. This is not a promise of identical execution or
performance on every broker. Unsupported or unsafe setups fail closed.
Symbols with prefixes/suffixes are resolved from the selected broker. Review
the displayed mapping. Exact overrides: -GoldSymbol "GOLD" -NasdaqSymbol
"US100.cash" -UsdJpySymbol "USDJPY.a". They must exist in the account's symbol
list and be fully tradeable cash instruments. No futures substitution.

PROMPTS AND DEFAULTS
1. Risk: fixed cash (default 50) or current-equity percentage (default 0.5%).
   Cash is denominated in ACCOUNT CURRENCY: 50 EUR on an EUR account, 50 JPY on
   a JPY account, not a converted USD50. Choose an appropriate amount.
2. Nasdaq DI ON/OFF (default ON).
3. USDJPY DI ON/OFF (default ON; ADX >=20 is retained).
4. Account mode: Challenge (+10%, default), Verification (+5%), or continuous
   demo/live (no profit-target entry stop; internal parameter name Funded).
   For ordinary demo testing choose 3. This does not certify a funded account.
5. Choose the already-running normal MT5 terminal/account and confirm details.

SAFETY / RISK
The reused Prop Ready guard caps planned stop risk at 0.5% of saved initial
balance, even when a higher amount is requested; source requested volume can
reduce it further. Lot sizes round down and trades below minimum size are
skipped, never forced. On a fresh 10,000 account, the planned cap is 50.
All limits scale with saved initial account-currency balance:
  Total retained initial risk 2.25%; per-symbol risk 1.5%.
  Daily new-entry/reserved-loss pause 3%; daily hard-loss latch 5%.
  Static hard equity floor 90%; projected admission equity buffer 92%.
  Seven entries / three net losing positions per Prague day; 3-second cooldown.
  Reserve 1.25 times retained initial risk plus 0.05% initial balance per open
  position and known negative swap; protected-equity margin cap 80%.
Stops pause NEW entries. Existing trades keep their original exits; they are
not forcibly liquidated. Gaps, slippage, fees and broker failures can exceed
planned risk and drawdown thresholds. This is not guaranteed prop compliance.

Challenge/Verification modes stop new entries at the respective balance target
after four distinct Prague entry days; existing trades must naturally flatten.
There is no automatic pass certification, automatic phase handover or account
switch. A new phase should use the new flat phase account and correct mode.

INSTALLATION AND REINSTALLATION
Before installing, log into the intended account, close all positions/pending
orders yourself, and turn Algo Trading OFF. The installer will not close trades.
It verifies the account again before changes, then gracefully restarts the
selected terminal with a Codex_Best_FTMO-<login>-<timestamp> nine-chart profile.
Algo Trading remains OFF. Inspect all charts/settings before enabling it yourself.
Never run this profile together with old copies, manual trades or another VPS.

The same binary package and account safety identity as Prop Ready are reused.
Both names share the saved Calyx-PropReady account/server baseline and guard
latches. Renaming or reinstalling does NOT reset drawdowns or clear safety
stops; do not delete that state to evade limits. Existing profiles are retained.
