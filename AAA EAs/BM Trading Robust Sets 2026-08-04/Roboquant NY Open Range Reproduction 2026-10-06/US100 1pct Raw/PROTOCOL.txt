Build the range from the first completed 15-minute bar opening at/after 09:30 America/New_York. One bar by default (09:30-09:45). RVOL = signal bar tick volume / mean of previous 20 completed M15 bar tick volumes EXCLUDING signal. Volume history continues overnight and across dates. Minimum RVOL 1.0. Clock semantics intentionally follow source: cutoff 15:00 and flat 15:55 compare to the CLOSED BAR'S OPEN timestamp. On a regular M15 grid, the first bar-open >=15:55 is 16:00, processed after its 16:15 close. This is not a 15:55 tick-timer exit. First tradable breakout bar normally opens 09:45 and closes at 10:00. NY UTC offset follows the actual US DST schedule; Exness server timestamp assumed UTC, matching existing USTEC research convention.

MT5 adaptation uses first available tick following M15 bar completion for the callback and native 150ms market execution delay. Bid OHLC signals and broker Bid/Ask fills replace futures prints. CFD sizing uses OrderCalcProfit for one lot from current executable Bid/Ask to rounded stop, floors to broker lot step, caps at broker maximum, and skips if below minimum.
