TIME AND SHARED INDICATORS
All rule clocks are America/Chicago, with historical US DST. Exness server
timestamps are treated as UTC, matching the existing Exness studies.
Daily/session ATR is NOT ATR(15): the source explicitly requires the 15-session
simple moving average of daily ATR. Sessions are midnight through 16:00 Chicago.
Underlying ATR period/type is unspecified: Wilder ATR(14) is OUR default.
Session OHLC is built from completed M30 bars, excluding bars at/after 16:00.
ATR uses gaps from the prior available session close, a 14-TR seed, and Wilder
recursion; the latest 15 completed session ATR readings define the day's level.
No-data sessions are omitted; available shortened holiday sessions are included.
Warm-up starts 240 calendar days before the native test's first tick. Indicator
state carries forward; it is not re-seeded or fit using the evaluation period.
Midnight VWAP = cumulative HLC3 * broker tick volume / cumulative tick volume,
using closed bars at each strategy's timeframe. Formula and CFD tick-volume
substitution are OUR implementation, not true exchange-traded volume.
ADX uses iADXWilder(14), closed-bar readings; period/type is OUR default.
Market orders are sent on the first tick of the next bar. Stop/target anchors
use its current executable Ask/Bid quote (not a future exact fill). No widening,
retry, fallback or forced minimum lot. Invalid levels or broker rejection skip.

1 — VAULT BREAK (source chapter 08:26; clarified 53:00 onwards)
M30, long only. Day's static upper noise barrier = midnight opening price +
0.30 * SMA15(session ATR). Midnight opening price is the first available M30
bar opening in the Chicago calendar day. Closed M30 close > barrier AND >
midnight VWAP. Next-bar order times 10:00 inclusive to 14:30 exclusive Chicago.
No crossing requirement: re-entry allowed after an exit, max 3 filled trades/day,
one position at a time. One-NQ-contract $1500 stop/$800 target translate to
75 / 40 Nasdaq price points; preserve those distances, NOT dollar loss amounts.
Flatten on first available tick at/after 14:30 Chicago. No trailing/filter added.

2 — VWAP PULLBACK + ADX (source chapter 22:34)
M1, long only. Opening range 08:30 inclusive to 09:00 exclusive Chicago.
Closed M1 close above OR high arms setup. A subsequent (or same) candle's low
touches VWAP; close back above VWAP records reclaim. ADX >20 AND ADX no greater
than its previous closed-bar value; gate may become valid on a later bar.
Current close must still be above VWAP when delayed ADX qualifies (OUR default).
Next-bar long; SL = lowest low last 20 completed M1 bars; TP = highest high
last 5 completed M1 bars, both INCLUDING signal candle. Reject if execution
quote is already beyond a target or invalid against broker constraints.
Flatten at 15:55 Chicago (as explicitly stated in the interview).
Gaps in published rules: entry cutoff, trade limit, VWAP anchor, state reset.
OUR frozen defaults: entry 10:00–14:30 Chicago, max 1 filled trade/day,
midnight VWAP; arm/touch/reclaim persist until fill or daily reset. No trailing.

3 — OVERNIGHT BIAS ORB (source chapter 35:50)
M15, both directions. Overnight range prior 23:00 to current 08:30 Chicago.
Freeze bias from the 08:30 opening quote: >= upper-third boundary long;
<= lower-third boundary short; inside middle third no trade. Boundary equality
and opens outside the overnight range follow the corresponding side (OUR detail).
First M15 bar 08:30–08:45 is OR. Later completed M15 close beyond OR high for
long bias or OR low for short bias AND ADX >20. Next-bar entry, 1 filled trade/day.
Opposite breaks don't invalidate frozen bias. SL = 0.30*SMA15(session ATR),
TP = 3*SL distance from quote. Flatten first available tick at/after 14:30 CT.
No trailing, ADX slope, news filter or overnight holding.
