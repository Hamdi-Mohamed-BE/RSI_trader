Returns include compounding and native costs. DD is the native tester’s maximum floating-equity drawdown. Sharpe uses UTC daily sampled equity returns × √365, including no-trade days, with zero risk-free rate; it is not MT5’s internal Sharpe. Win/loss streaks count complete net-closed positions. Net R divides each realised net trade by its USD initial fill-to-stop risk; gaps, fees and time exits make realised outcomes differ from ±1R. Tick-quality percentages do not turn generated ticks into genuine historical ticks.

Important sizing limitation: after severe drawdown, some accounts cannot meet the broker’s minimum lot at 1% risk. Those signals are skipped, never rounded up. A longer test can therefore contain fewer trades than a fresh one-year account; see each asset’s undersize/margin count and filled-trade dates below.

Frozen reconstruction rules

TimeframeCompleted M5 candles; enter at next available tick, never signal close fill.
VwapUTC 00:00 daily reset. Sum of completed M5 HLC3 times broker tick volume divided by cumulative tick volume, INCLUDING signal bar. Minimum 15 positive-volume bars. Same anchor on every asset.
SignalFirst completed close with (VWAP-close)/ATR14 >= 2 while armed; no reversal/bullish/RSI/DI/ADX/news filter.
RearmAfter a completed close back inside the 2-ATR boundary or new UTC session. One attempt per excursion; signals while a position is open skipped, no queue.
Stop1x completed signal M5 ATR14 below requested ask entry; rounded to nearest broker tick.
Target1x actual requested-entry-to-rounded-stop distance above requested ask entry. Fixed 1R, NOT a full return to VWAP.
SessionAll broker tradable sessions, entries through 23:50 UTC; flat on first available tick >=23:55 UTC, or next UTC day if no closing tick. Crypto weekends included.
ManagementNo trailing, partials, breakeven, pyramiding or opposite-side hedge. One open position per independent asset account.
Sizing1% current closed balance nominal stop risk. OrderCalcProfit converts to USD; floor broker lot step. Skip below minimum size, invalid stops or margin constraint.
CostsNative broker bid/ask, commission, swap and 150ms delay. Stops/targets sent with order relative to requested quote; realised R can differ due to fills/gaps/costs.

The broker clock UTC+0 matches the official Exness server-time documentation. The UTC daily anchor is not US regular-hours or futures-session VWAP. CFD tick volume is not exchange traded volume.
