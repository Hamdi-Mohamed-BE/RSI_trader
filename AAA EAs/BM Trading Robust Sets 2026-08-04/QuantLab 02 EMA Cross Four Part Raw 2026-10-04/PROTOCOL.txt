FROZEN BEFORE ANY BACKTEST: 2026-10-04, independent NQBlade-inspired concept.
Not QuantLab's proprietary executable or exact published strategy settings.
Public verified concept: https://api-quantlab.com/algorithmen/nqblade
Nasdaq M3 EMA-cross, entry confirmation, four portions/three targets plus runner.
Numerical settings are unavailable publicly. Everything numeric below is our assumption.
No verified existing EA follows this combination. Standalone tests; no unrelated EA comparison.

SIGNAL: completed M3 EMA(9) crosses EMA(21). Cross requires prior bar fast<=slow then fast>slow
for buy, reverse for sell. Signal-bar ADX(14)>=20, +DI>-DI for buy, -DI>+DI for sell.
Only closed bars/indicators, no delayed confirmation or lookahead.
Signal candles open 09:30 inclusive to 15:30 exclusive New York, weekdays.
Entry next bar first tradable tick (with 150ms native execution delay).
Maximum one basket per New York entry date; no new basket while any portion remains open.

INITIAL STOP: common basket SL at 2*ATR(14 M3) from initial requested entry quote,
aligned to tick size and widened only if necessary to meet actual broker stop/freeze distance.
FOUR PORTIONS: four hedged positions, each assigned one quarter of the cash-risk budget.
Portions 1/2/3: nominal TP 1R/2R/3R from each portion's pre-order quote to common stop.
Actual fills may differ due to execution delay; actual realized initial RR is not forced to equal nominal.
Runner portion: no TP. After its favourable move reaches +1 initial R, latch trailing active;
on completed M3 bars, trail at closed-bar EMA21, never widen, respect broker gap.
No break-even rule on target portions. No opposite-cross liquidation.
BOUNDARIES (assumptions): close all surviving portions after 24 hours at next tradable tick,
and on Friday at/after 15:55 NY. No weekend holding. Monday-Thursday overnight holds possible.

RISK: 1% of current account equity per entire basket, not per portion. Deposit USD 10,000.
Broker lot sizing rounded DOWN. Skip entire basket if its initial quarter-risk lot cannot meet
minimum volume. No minimum-lot risk override in this research-only prototype.
Actual fills/gaps and costs can exceed planned risk; no guarantee of a hard maximum loss.
Partial entry failure: flatten already opened legs and flag test invalid; never manufacture fills.
Broker hedging account required. EA refuses non-tester execution.
No martingale, grid, averaging down or portfolio/adaptive controls.

NATIVE TESTS: Exness-MT5Trial16 USTEC M3, 1:2000 leverage, model 4 (every tick based on real ticks),
execution delay 150ms, native spread/commission/swap. No invented spread or cost stress.
Smoke: 2026-09-20 inclusive through 2026-10-04 exclusive.
1Y: 2025-10-04 inclusive through 2026-10-04 exclusive.
3M: 2026-07-04 inclusive through 2026-10-04 exclusive.
Each test fresh/flat; recent window not copied from tail of the yearly run.
Real ticks available from 2026-01-01; earlier part of yearly test generated ticks.
All end-of-test liquidations stay in headline results and are separately identified.

STATISTICS: primary trade count, win rate, net PF and streaks measured per full four-portion basket.
Native position statistics are separately shown as leg statistics, not inflated basket win rates.
P&L includes all four legs, commissions and swaps. Equity DD is MT5 native floating-equity DD.
Closed-balance graphs book every leg at its actual closing timestamp, not just basket completion.
No parameter optimisation, holdout validation, Monte Carlo or full-pipeline approval at this step.
No production EA, live terminal, BAT, website, Git commit or remote changed.
Stop after this standalone idea and wait for user review before the next algorithm.
