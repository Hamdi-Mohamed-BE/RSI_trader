Evidence and limits

XAU Trend Progression: 75% real ticks; EMA3 Gold: 75% real ticks; Asia Breakout Gold: 75% real ticks; USDJPY London Momentum: 75% real ticks; XAU RSI VWAP: 75% real ticks

PF / win rate are net of commission and swap. Equity DD is native relative floating-equity drawdown; charts show closed balance only. Sharpe¹ is annualized calendar-day closed-balance returns, including zero days (zero risk-free rate), not native trade-level Sharpe.

Gate rejects count attempted candidates on each variant’s own exposure path. Trade Δ compares actual trade count with baseline, not a direct rejected subset. Minimum/upward lot rounding can exceed the target risk. Separate runs are not a shared portfolio.

Screen requires ≥30 trades, ≥50% of baseline trades, PF≥1.20 and ≥baseline+0.05, positive net, return≥baseline and equity DD≤baseline. Passing this screen is not approval to deploy. Holdout / rolling tests, cost stress and Monte Carlo remain outstanding.

All five default-off parity checks passed. 1822 trade records and 1488 entry fills crosschecked. Source, SET and original compiled hashes unchanged.
