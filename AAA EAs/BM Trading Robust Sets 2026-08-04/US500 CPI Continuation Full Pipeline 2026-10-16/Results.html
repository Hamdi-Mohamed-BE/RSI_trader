Trading rules held fixed

  1. Use the documented CPI schedule at 08:30 New York, DST aware. Headline/core releases are one event. No actual-versus-consensus filter: no verified point-in-time consensus archive is assumed.
  2. Pre-event reference: exact last completed M1 close. ATR uses 14 true ranges from 15 consecutive M1 bars strictly before the release.
  3. Only the fixed first release-aligned minute defines the impulse. Require sufficient close displacement versus the pre-event ATR and a close in the directional outer quartile.
  4. Enter a continuation only on an executable breakout of that first candle’s extreme after its close and within the entry deadline. Stop beyond its opposite extreme plus 0.1 pre-event ATR and one tick.
  5. Selected target/holding/impulse settings are displayed above. One signal attempt per event; quote freshness ≤2 seconds, positive entry spread, spread ≤10% of stop distance, downward-rounded broker lot sizing. No forced fallback entry.
  6. No trailing, partials or break-even. Time-close at the configured holding deadline; hard-flat by release +60 minutes at an executable quote. Tester-only guard; no live operation.

Predeclared search and data splits

One factor at a time · beam width one

  1. Target candidates: 0.5R, 1R, 1.5R, 2R.
  2. Maximum hold: 5, 15, 30 minutes.
  3. Initial displacement threshold: 0.75, 1.0, 1.25 × pre-event ATR.
  4. Probe the selected centre and original baseline neighbours: target ±0.25R, hold ±5 minutes, impulse ±0.25 ATR within frozen bounds.

At most two finalists—the development plateau and unchanged baseline—reach the predeclared validation selection. Validation eligibility requires at least six trades, positive net, PF >1, and a profitable development-neighbour majority. If neither qualifies, retain the development fallback and explicitly fail validation; do not choose a new final-evaluation winner.

No search on the final evaluation period. Actual unique and reused trials are counted in the audited register, not estimated from Cartesian combinations.
