## Setup (HTF = M15, SOURCE: "we're using the 15-minute time frame as our reference")
- C1 = reference candle (M15 shift 2), C2 = sweep candle (shift 1, just closed), C3 = the current M15 candle (signal window).
- SOURCE (page): C1 qualifies only if |body| >= 50% of its range.
- SELL: C1 bullish (ASSUMPTION: mirror of the stated buy), C2 high > C1 high (CRH) and C2 close < CRH (and > CRL).

- Midline = (CRH + CRL) / 2 (SOURCE page: Midline 50%). Premium = at/above midline (sell zone), discount = at/below (buy zone).
- SOURCE (page): "The setup is confirmed when LTF price tests the Premium or Discount zone." ASSUMPTION: only during C3; unfired setups expire at C3's close.
