DMC video reconstruction: frozen, research-only comparison

Open Results.html for the three-year and recent-window comparison. The 28 native
cases, trades, matching settings and compressed tester reports are included.
PLAN.json records the approved reconstruction assumptions and video attribution.
BUILD.json and VERIFICATION.json record the model/evidence checks at study time.
The live DMC experts and portfolio membership were not changed by this study.

Private tester.ini files, terminal journals and the full third-party transcript
remain local and are not published. verify.py can check the published report and
setting hashes using the repository's existing control experts. Recompilation
requires the original caption file named in run.py; its digest is in BUILD.json.
Regenerating make_report.py's journal-derived diagnostics requires the private
local journals. Results.html and COMPARISON.json already contain the extracted
diagnostics without publishing account-bound terminal logs.

These are retrospective raw tests, not untouched out-of-sample validation.
Real ticks begin in January 2026; older periods include generated ticks.
