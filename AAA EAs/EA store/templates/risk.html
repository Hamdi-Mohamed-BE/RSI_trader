Risk disclosure

Automation removes hesitation. It does not remove risk.

Read this before purchasing, installing or running any EA.

!

You can lose all deposited capital.

Leveraged CFDs, metals, indices, crypto and stocks can gap or move through stops. Historical drawdown is not a maximum future drawdown.

No guaranteed performance

No EA, backtest, optimization, locked validation or live result guarantees future profit, a prop-firm payout, or preservation of capital. Market regimes change and a previously profitable rule can stop working.

Backtests are models

Results depend on data quality, modelling method, spread, commission, swap, slippage, execution delay and broker contract specifications. Research using CFD tick activity is not the same as centralized exchange volume.

Portfolio risk can stack

The current installer can open many independent positions. One percent per EA is not one percent total portfolio risk. Correlated gold, Nasdaq and equity setups can lose together.

The combined audit is an archived overlay

The portfolio audit combines realized cash flows from separate EA tests using the previous configuration. It has not been revalidated for the new ADX/DI filters and is not a native simultaneous multi-EA simulation. Shared margin, floating drawdown and correlated exposure can be worse in live trading. Do not treat the complete package as a pre-validated live portfolio.

Customer responsibility
